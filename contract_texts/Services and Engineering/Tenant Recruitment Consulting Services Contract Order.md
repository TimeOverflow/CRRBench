# Tenant Recruitment Consulting Services Contract Order

Party A: [Chenghong Technology (Hangzhou) Co., Ltd. ] Party B: [Jiahe Digital Industry Technology Co., Ltd. ]

This Order is issued under the Tenant Recruitment Consulting Services Contract (the "Original Contract") entered into on March 10, 2026 by Chenghong Technology (Hangzhou) Co., Ltd. (the "Project Company") and Party B. Under the Original Contract, Party B recommends projects of the Project Company and its affiliates to prospective tenants. The relevant affiliate and Party B confirm the service fee by signing an order such as this Order. The Original Contract governs the parties' respective rights and obligations. Party A is an affiliate of the Project Company.

Party A and Party B accept to cooperate in accordance with the original contract. After consultation and consensus, Party A and Party B confirm as follows:

# I. Scope of Cooperation:

1. Party B recommends investment customers for Party A, and promotes customers to sign a lease contract with Party A for the [Yungang Science and Technology Innovation Center] project operated by Party A.

1. The service fee for the transactions with the customers identified above is RMB 128,860.00 (in words: One Hundred Twenty-Eight Thousand Eight Hundred Sixty Renminbi Only).

# III. Rights and Obligations of the Parties:

1. Party B shall ensure that information supplied about prospective tenants is accurate and lawfully obtained, and shall assist Party A with tenant introductions and negotiations.

2. Party B acknowledges that Party A may pay the service fee after receiving a lawful and valid Exclusive Authorization and other required documents under this Order and the Original Contract. If Party B fails to provide a lawful and valid Exclusive Authorization, and Party A has already paid Party B a service fee for a customer's transaction, Party B shall resolve any third-party claims against Party A for brokerage fees, service fees, commissions or similar charges for that transaction.

3. If Party B and a third party each claim to have introduced the same customer, the lawful and valid Exclusive Authorization signed by the customer shall control. Party B shall refund to Party A's account the service fee received for the disputed customer within five working days after Party A gives notice.

4. This Order prevails to the extent it conflicts with the Original Contract. The Original Contract governs matters that this Order does not address.

# IV. Confidentiality:

Both parties have the obligation to keep confidential the trade secrets and undisclosed information of the other party learned through the signing and performance of this Order and the original contract, and shall not disclose them to third parties without the written consent of the other party, except as otherwise provided by laws and regulations or required by the regulatory authorities.

# V. Liability for Breach:

If any party violates this Order or the original contract and causes losses to the other party, it shall bear the corresponding liability for compensation according to law.

# VI. Anti-Bribery:

In entering into and performing this Order, both parties shall comply strictly with national laws and regulations against commercial bribery. Neither party may offer improper benefits in any form to, or accept them in any form from, the other party's personnel or any relevant third party. If either party breaches this clause, the non-breaching party may terminate this Order and seek compensation for its losses.

# VII. Dispute Resolution:

The validity, interpretation and dispute settlement of this Order shall be governed by the laws of the People's Republic of China, and the dispute resolution method agreed in the original contract shall prevail. Disputes arising from this Order shall be resolved by amicable negotiations between the two parties; if the negotiation fails, either party shall have the right to file a lawsuit in the people's court with jurisdiction over the defendant's location.

# VIII. Miscellaneous:

1. This Order shall be stamped by Party A and Party B. It shall be made in duplicate, one copy for each party, and shall have the same legal effect.

2. The Original Contract governs matters that this Order does not address.
