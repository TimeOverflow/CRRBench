Huayuan Aviation Supplies Co., Ltd.

National online car-hailing service project

Procurement contract

Party A: Huayuan Aviation Supplies Co., Ltd.

Party B: Jin'an Mobility Technology Co., Ltd.

According to the Civil Code of the People's Republic of China and other relevant laws and regulations, Party A and Party B reached an agreement on Party A's procurement of Party B's national online car-hailing service after equal consultation and signed this Contract.

Article 1 Scope of Services

1. Party B provides Party A with online car-hailing services covering major cities across the country, including online appointment, dispatching and settlement services for employee travel cars, airport transfers, business trips and other scenarios.

2. Party B shall ensure that the vehicles and drivers providing services have legal operating qualifications. For service standards, please refer to Annex IV "Measures for the Assessment of Online Car-hailing Service".

3. Party B should provide Party A with a car management background through its operating platform to support order inquiry, cost return and data export.

Article 2 Service Term

This Contract is valid from July 1, 2026 to June 30, 2028. Thirty days before the expiration of the term, the two parties can negotiate for renewal.

Article 3 Service Fees and Settlement

1. The online car-hailing service is settled according to the actual mileage, duration and city unit price. For the unit price standard, please refer to Annex I "Service Price List".

2. Both parties settle accounts according to the natural month. Party B shall provide Party A with the details and bills of the previous month's car use within ten working days after the end of each month. After Party A checks that it is correct, Party B will issue an invoice, and Party A shall pay within 30 working days after receiving the compliant invoice.

3. Party B issues a special VAT invoice with an applicable tax rate of 6%. Party B collection account information:

   Account Bank: Tianjin Heping Branch of China Construction Bank Co., Ltd.
   Bank Account No.: 12050166889052500321

Article 4 Rights and Obligations of Party A

1. Party A shall settle and pay the online car-hailing service fee on time.

2. Party A has the right to supervise Party B's service quality, vehicle and driver qualifications, and require Party B to rectify.

3. Party A has the right to adjust the service city and the scale of the vehicle according to the actual vehicle demand, and the specific adjustment method shall be implemented in accordance with Article 9, Paragraph 3 of this Contract.

Article 5 Rights and Obligations of Party B

1. Party B shall provide safe, punctual and high-quality online car-hailing services in accordance with the agreed service standards to ensure that the qualifications of vehicles and drivers are legal and valid.

2. Party B should establish a perfect dispatching, customer service and emergency response mechanism to deal with Party A employees' car needs and complaints in a timely manner.

3. Party B shall be responsible for the labor, social security and safety responsibilities of its drivers. Party B shall be fully responsible for accidents and losses caused by Party B or the driver.

4. Party A has the right to transfer all or part of the rights and obligations under this Contract to its affiliates without the consent of Party B; without the prior written consent of Party A, Party B shall not transfer or subcontract all or part of the rights and obligations under this Contract to a third party, otherwise Party A has the right to terminate this Contract.

Article 6 Confidentiality

Both parties have the obligation to keep confidential the other party's trade secrets, employee personal information and undisclosed information known through the signing and performance of this Contract, and shall not disclose them to third parties without the written consent of the other party, except as otherwise provided by laws and regulations or required by the regulatory authorities. The obligation of this article shall not be invalidated by the cancellation or termination of this Contract.

Article 7 Liability for Breach

1. If the services provided by Party B do not meet the agreed standards or a safety accident occurs, Party A has the right to require Party B to rectify within a time limit; if the circumstances are serious, Party A has the right to terminate this Contract, and Party B shall refund the corresponding fees collected and pay Party A liquidated damages at 30% of the total annual service of this Contract.

2. If Party B discloses Party A's trade secrets or its employees' personal information, Party B shall compensate Party A for all resulting losses and separately pay liquidated damages of RMB 500,000.00 (in words: Five Hundred Thousand Renminbi Only).

3. If Party B delivers the settlement details late or an interruption in services affects Party A's use, Party B shall pay Party A liquidated damages equal to 0.2% of that month's service fees for each day of delay.

4. If Party A pays the service fee late, it shall pay liquidated damages to Party B at 0.03% of the unpaid amount for each day of delay.

Article 8 Force Majeure

If one party is unable to perform this Contract due to force majeure such as earthquakes, typhoons, floods, fires, wars, epidemics, government acts, etc., the encountering party shall notify the other party in writing in time and provide proof within a reasonable period of time. The two parties shall not be responsible for each other, but shall take necessary measures to prevent the expansion of losses.

Article 9 Anti-Bribery

In entering into and performing this Contract, both parties shall comply strictly with national laws and regulations against commercial bribery. Neither party may offer improper benefits in any form to, or accept them in any form from, the other party's personnel or any relevant third party. If either party breaches this clause, the non-breaching party may terminate this Contract and seek compensation for its losses.

Article 10 Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

1. The validity, interpretation, change, execution and dispute settlement of this Contract shall be governed by the laws of the People's Republic of China.

2. Any dispute arising out of this Contract or related to this Contract shall be resolved by amicable negotiations between the two parties; if the negotiation fails, either party shall have the right to file a lawsuit in the people's court with jurisdiction over the defendant's location.

Article 11 Effectiveness and Miscellaneous Provisions

1. This Contract takes effect on the date both parties affix their seals. It is executed in four counterparts of equal legal effect, two for each party.

2. For matters not covered by this Contract, the two parties may sign a written supplementary agreement separately, which shall have the same legal effect as this Contract.
