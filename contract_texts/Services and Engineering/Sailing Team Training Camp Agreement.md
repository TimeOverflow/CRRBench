In view of the fact that Party A intends to entrust Party B to carry out sailing team training camp training for its employees, and Party B has the corresponding training qualifications and venue conditions, Party A and Party B have reached the following agreement on team training camp training after equal consultation:

I. Training content

1.  Party B provides sailing team training camp training services for Party A employees, including theoretical teaching, water practice, teamwork training and safety protection.

2.  The training location is the Shendi City Sailing Center operated by Party B. Please refer to Annex I "Training Schedule" for the training period and number of people.

3.  Party B shall ensure that the training coach has the corresponding qualifications and the training facilities meet the safety standards.

II. Training period

The training service of this Agreement will be carried out in four phases from August 1, 2026 to October 31, 2026, each period will be carried out for three days.

III. Training fee and payment

1.  The total training fee under this Agreement is RMB 186,000.00 (in words: One Hundred Eighty-Six Thousand Renminbi Only), with settlement based on actual costs after each training session concludes.

2.  Party A shall pay the current fee within 20 working days after receiving the compliance invoice issued by the service party after the end of each training and acceptance.

IV. Rights and Obligations of Party A

1.  Party A shall organize the training staff as agreed and pay the training fee on time.

2.  Party A should cooperate with Party B to carry out safety notification and health application before training.

3.  Party A has the right to supervise the quality of training and make reasonable suggestions to Party B on training arrangements.

V. Rights and Obligations of Party B

1.  Party B shall provide training services in accordance with the agreed training content, standards and safety requirements.

2.  Party B shall purchase personal accident insurance for the participants during the training period, and be equipped with professional coaches and lifeguards.

3.  Party B shall ensure that the training site, equipment and facilities meet the safety standards. Party B shall bear the corresponding responsibility for the personal or property damage of the participants due to Party B.

4.  The service party shall properly keep the information of the participants provided by Party A and shall not use it for purposes other than this Agreement without the written consent of Party A.

VI. Confidentiality

Both parties shall have the obligation to keep confidential the trade secrets and undisclosed information of the other party learned through the signing and performance of this Agreement, and shall not disclose them to third parties without the written consent of the other party, except as otherwise provided by laws and regulations. After the termination of this Agreement, the confidentiality obligations of both parties shall not be terminated.

VII. Liability for Breach

1.  If any party violates this Agreement and causes losses to the other party, it shall bear the corresponding liability for compensation in accordance with the law.

2.  If Party B fails to provide training services as agreed or the training quality does not meet the standard, Party A has the right to request Party B to rectify or refund the corresponding fee within a time limit.

3.  If Party A pays the training fee late, it shall pay liquidated damages to Party B at 0.05% of the unpaid amount for each day of delay.

VIII. Force Majeure

If one party is unable to perform this Agreement due to force majeure such as earthquakes, typhoons, floods, fires, wars, epidemics, government acts, etc., the party shall notify the other party in writing in a timely manner and provide proof within a reasonable period of time, and the two parties shall not be responsible for each other.

IX. Anti-Bribery

In entering into and performing this Agreement, both parties shall comply strictly with national laws and regulations against commercial bribery. Neither party may offer improper benefits in any form to, or accept them in any form from, the other party's personnel or any relevant third party. If either party breaches this clause, the non-breaching party may terminate this Agreement and seek compensation for its losses.

X. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

1.  The validity, interpretation, change, execution and dispute settlement of this Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the People's Republic of China.

2.  Any dispute arising out of or related to this Agreement shall be resolved by amicable negotiations between the two parties; if the negotiation fails, either party has the right to file a lawsuit in the people's court with jurisdiction over the location of Party A.

XI. Miscellaneous

1.  This Agreement shall take effect from the date of seal of both parties. It shall be made in duplicate, and Party A and Party B shall have one copy each, which shall have the same legal effect.

2.  For matters not covered by this Agreement, the two parties may sign a separate written supplementary agreement.

3.  Without the prior written consent of the other party, neither party shall transfer the rights and obligations under this Agreement to a third party.
