In view of the fact that Party A intends to provide enterprise car services to its employees, and Party B has the corresponding online car-hailing operation qualifications and service capabilities, Party A and Party B reached the following agreement on enterprise car service matters after equal consultation:

I. Scope of Services

1. Party B provides Party A with online car-hailing appointment, dispatching, settlement and related services for corporate employees' commuting, business travel and other scenarios.

2. Party B shall ensure that the vehicles and drivers providing services have legal operating qualifications and comply with the regulations of the transportation management department.

3. For specific service standards, response time limit and service quality requirements, please refer to Annex II "Car Service Standards".

II. Service Term

This Agreement is valid from June 1, 2026 to May 31, 2027. Thirty days before the expiration of the term, the two parties can negotiate for renewal.

III. Service Fees and Settlement

1. The enterprise car service is settled according to the actual mileage and unit price. For the unit price standard, please refer to Annex I Service Price List.

2. Both parties settle accounts according to the natural month. Party B shall provide Party A with the details of the previous month's car use within ten working days after the end of each month. After Party A checks that it is correct, Party B will issue an invoice, and Party A shall pay within 30 working days after receiving the compliant invoice.

IV. Rights and Obligations of Party A

1. Party A shall settle and pay the car service fee on time.

2. Party A has the right to supervise Party B's service quality, vehicle and driver qualifications, and require Party B to rectify.

3. Party A shall properly keep the service data provided by Party B and shall not be used for purposes other than this Agreement without the written consent of Party B.

V. Rights and Obligations of Party B

1. Party B shall provide safe, punctual and high-quality vehicle services in accordance with Annex II's Vehicle Service Standards, and ensure that the vehicle and driver qualifications are legal and valid.

2. Party B should establish a perfect dispatching, customer service and emergency response mechanism to deal with Party A employees' car needs and complaints in a timely manner.

3. Party B shall ensure that the personal information collected and used in the course of its service is legal and compliant, and abide by the relevant regulations on data security and personal information protection.

3. Party B shall be responsible for the labor, social security and safety responsibilities of its drivers. Party B shall be fully responsible for accidents and losses caused by Party B or the driver.

VI. Confidentiality

1. Party B shall maintain strict confidentiality over Party A's trade secrets, employee personal information and travel data, and over the contents of this Agreement, that Party B learns in connection with entering into or performing this Agreement. Party B shall not disclose any such information to a third party without Party A's written consent. Party B shall pay Party A liquidated damages of RMB 200,000.00 (in words: Two Hundred Thousand Renminbi Only) for each breach of this clause. If the liquidated damages are insufficient to cover Party A's losses, Party B shall compensate the shortfall. Party A may also terminate this Agreement.

2. Party B shall take security protection measures such as encryption and desensitization for data involving the travel of Party A employees in accordance with Annex II "Enterprise Vehicle Service Specification".

3. The confidentiality obligations stipulated in this article are not limited by the term of this Agreement and are still valid for five years after the termination of this Agreement.

VII. Liability for Breach

1. If the services provided by Party B do not meet the agreed standards or a safety accident occurs, Party A has the right to require Party B to rectify within a time limit; if the circumstances are serious, Party A has the right to terminate this Agreement, and Party B shall refund the corresponding fees collected and pay Party A liquidated damages at 20% of the total annual services under this Agreement.

2. If Party B discloses Party A's trade secrets or employee's personal information, Party B shall compensate Party A for all losses and pay an additional liquidated damages of RMB 200,000.00.

3. If Party B delivers the settlement details late or an interruption in services affects Party A's use, Party B shall pay Party A liquidated damages equal to 0.2% of that month's service fees for each day of delay.

4. If Party A pays the service fee late, it shall pay liquidated damages to Party B at 0.03% of the unpaid amount for each day of delay.

VIII. Force Majeure

If one party is unable to perform this Agreement due to force majeure such as earthquakes, typhoons, floods, fires, wars, epidemics, government acts, etc., the party shall notify the other party in writing in a timely manner and provide proof within a reasonable period of time, and the two parties shall not be responsible for each other.

IX. Anti-Bribery

In entering into and performing this Agreement, both parties shall comply strictly with national laws and regulations against commercial bribery. Neither party may offer improper benefits in any form to, or accept them in any form from, the other party's personnel or any relevant third party. If either party breaches this clause, the non-breaching party may terminate this Agreement and seek compensation for its losses.

X. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

1. The validity, interpretation, change, execution and dispute settlement of this Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the People's Republic of China.

2. Any dispute arising out of or related to this Agreement shall be resolved by amicable negotiations between the two parties; if the negotiation fails, either party has the right to file a lawsuit in the people's court with jurisdiction over the location of Party A.

XI. Effectiveness and Miscellaneous Provisions

1. This Agreement shall take effect from the date of seal of both parties. It shall be made in duplicate, and Party A and Party B shall have one copy each, which shall have the same legal effect.

2. For matters not covered by this Agreement, the two parties may sign a separate written supplementary agreement.

3. Without the prior written consent of the other party, neither party shall transfer the rights and obligations under this Agreement to a third party.
