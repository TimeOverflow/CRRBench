# 折 让 协 议

# Discount Agreement

日期 Date:  2026 年 04 月 18 日

华晨汽车制造有限公司（以下简称"甲方"）与 云图软件有限公司 公司（以下简称"乙方" ）经过协商，乙方同意向甲方折让 600,000.00 RMB（不含税），作为对乙方在 2026 年01 月01 日 到 2026 年03 月31 日 期间向甲方供应以下产品的价格拆让。此协议可作为双方 财务办理相关手续的通知。

Through negotiation between  Huachen Automobile Manufacturing Co., Ltd. （Part A）and    CloudMap Software Co., Ltd.  （Part B）, Party B should give a discount in total   600,000.00   RMB without VAT to Party A as the material discount for the following parts which Party B delivered to Party A during   2026-01-01 to   2026-03-31  This agreement could be the notice for both parties financial department to carry out related procedure.

折让明细 Discount Details:

| 序号 No. | 物料编码 Part No. | 物料名称 Part Name | 折让金额 Discount Amount (RMB) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | CM-NAV-2026 | 车载导航地图数据 In-vehicle navigation map data | 380,000.00 |
| 2 | CM-POI-2026 | 兴趣点数据更新服务 POI data update service | 220,000.00 |

合计 Total: 600,000.00 RMB（不含税 without VAT）

1. 本折让协议系针对乙方在上述期间向甲方供应产品的价格折让，折让金额为不含税金额，甲方应据此调减相应应付账款。
This Discount Agreement is made in respect of the price discount for the products supplied by Party B to Party A during the above period. The discount amount is exclusive of VAT, and Party A shall accordingly reduce the corresponding accounts payable.

2. 乙方应在本折让协议签署后十五个工作日内，按折让金额办理相应的财务冲减手续，并配合甲方完成账务调整。
Party B shall, within fifteen (15) working days after the signing of this Discount Agreement, complete the corresponding financial write-off procedures for the discount amount and cooperate with Party A in completing the account adjustment.

3. 本折让协议一式两份，甲乙双方各执一份，经双方盖章后生效，作为双方财务办理相关手续的依据。
This Discount Agreement is made in two originals, with each party holding one copy. It shall become effective upon being stamped by both parties and shall serve as the basis for both parties' financial departments to carry out related procedures.
