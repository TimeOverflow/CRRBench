Network and Data Security and Personal Information Protection Agreement

Party A: Huatong Communication Group Co., Ltd

Party B: Tianshu Yunxin Technology Co., Ltd. (referred to as "Tianshu Yunxin")

In view of the fact that Party A and Party B carried out business cooperation between Huatong and Tianshu Yunxin in 2026 (referred to as "business cooperation"), the two parties hereby agree on the network and data security and personal information protection matters involved in the cooperation as follows:

Article 1  Party B undertakes to comply with the Cybersecurity Law of the People's Republic of China, the Data Security Law of the People's Republic of China, the Personal Information Protection Law of the People's Republic of China, and all applicable laws, regulations, rules, regulatory instruments issued by competent authorities, and mandatory national and industry standards relating to cybersecurity, data security and personal information protection (the "Applicable Legal Requirements"). Party B shall use networks lawfully, establish and maintain appropriate cybersecurity, data security and personal information protection systems, and implement cybersecurity classification and protection, technical safeguards, assessments, reporting, monitoring and early warning measures. Party B shall ensure secure and stable network operation and preserve the integrity, confidentiality and availability of network data.

Article 2  Party B undertakes that it, its personnel who perform the business cooperation, and any third parties and their personnel with whom it must work to perform the cooperation (collectively, "Party B and Related Personnel") shall understand and comply with the rules and policies on network and data security and personal information protection applicable to Party A's business partners. They shall not use assets under Party A's administration or its brands, trade names, trademarks, marks, logos or similar information in violation of applicable laws or regulations.

Article 3  Data Security Requirements

1.Party B that collects, stores, uses, processes, transmits, provides and discloses personal information and important data in business cooperation shall abide by the principles of legality, legitimacy, necessity and good faith, specify the purpose, method and scope of processing, and obtain the separate consent of the relevant personal information subject.

2.Party B shall take security technical measures such as encryption, de-identification and access control to prevent personal information and important data from being tampered, destroyed, leaked or illegally obtained and illegally used.

3.Party B shall store personal information and important data collected and generated in business cooperation in China; if it really needs to be provided abroad, it shall pass the security assessment in accordance with the law.

4.If or may cause the leakage, tampering or loss of personal information, Party B shall immediately take remedial measures, inform the relevant subjects in accordance with the provisions and report to the relevant competent authorities.

Article 4  Rights and Obligations of Party A

1.Party A has the right to supervise and inspect Party B's performance of this Agreement, and Party B shall cooperate.

2.Party A shall provide the necessary interface and technical support for Party B to carry out business cooperation.

3.Party A has the right to require Party B to provide management systems, technical plans and compliance certification materials related to network and data security.

Article 5  Rights and Obligations of Party B

1.Party B shall carry out business cooperation in accordance with this Agreement and relevant laws to ensure the security of the network and data.

2.Party B shall designate the person in charge of network and data security and inform Party A in writing.

3.If Party B's breach of this Agreement causes Party A to incur losses, face sanctions from a competent authority, or become subject to a claim from a relevant person or entity, Party B shall pay Party A liquidated damages of RMB 500,000 and compensate Party A for all resulting losses. If Party B fails to remedy the breach on time, Party B shall pay Party A additional liquidated damages of 0.5% of the total contract amount for each day of delay.

3.Party B shall assist Party A in handling requests for inquiry, copying, correction, deletion, etc. of the subject of relevant personal information.

Article 6  Confidentiality

Both parties have the obligation to keep confidential the trade secrets, technical information and undisclosed information of the other party known through the signing and performance of this Agreement, and shall not disclose them to third parties without the written consent of the other party, except as otherwise provided by laws and regulations or required by the regulatory authorities. The obligation of this article shall not be invalidated by the cancellation or termination of this Agreement.

Article 7  Liability for Breach

1.If any party violates this Agreement and causes losses to the other party, it shall bear the corresponding liability for compensation in accordance with the law.

2.If Party B has a data security accident or violates the provisions of Article 3 and Article 5 of this Agreement, Party A has the right to immediately suspend or terminate the business cooperation and require Party B to bear the corresponding responsibility.

Article 8  Anti-Bribery

In entering into and performing this Agreement, both parties shall comply strictly with national laws and regulations against commercial bribery. Neither party may offer improper benefits in any form to, or accept them in any form from, the other party's personnel or any relevant third party. If either party breaches this clause, the non-breaching party may terminate this Agreement and seek compensation for its losses.

Article 9  Term and Termination

1.This Agreement shall take effect from the date of the seal of both parties, and the validity period shall be the same as the term of business cooperation.

2.If the business cooperation is terminated or this Agreement is terminated, Party B shall delete or anonymously process the relevant personal information and important data in accordance with the requirements of Party A, and confirm it in writing.

Article 10  Force Majeure

If one party is unable to perform this Agreement due to force majeure such as earthquakes, typhoons, floods, fires, wars, epidemics, government actions or large-scale network attacks, the affected party shall notify the other party in writing in a timely manner and provide proof within a reasonable period of time. Both parties shall not be responsible for each other, but shall take necessary measures to prevent the expansion of losses.

Article 11  Miscellaneous

1.For matters not covered by this Agreement, the two parties may sign a written supplementary agreement separately, and the supplementary agreement shall have the same legal effect as this Agreement.

2.The validity, interpretation and dispute settlement of this Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the People's Republic of China. Any dispute arising out of this Agreement or related to this Agreement shall be resolved by amicable negotiations between the two parties; if the negotiation fails, either party shall have the right to file a lawsuit in the people's court with jurisdiction over the location of Party A.

3.This Agreement shall be made in duplicate, and Party A and Party B shall each hold one copy, which shall have the same legal effect.
