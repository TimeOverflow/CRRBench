# Qingyou Space Influencer Barter Cooperation Agreement

Party A:
Zhejiang Qingyou Zhilu Technology Co., Ltd.
Party B:
Zhao Xiaonan

In view of:

1. Party B owns the social media account "Nande You Ni" (account ID: 208857331), which has 150,000 followers. The account covers visits to local businesses and life in the workplace. Its audience consists primarily of urban professionals and entrepreneurs and aligns closely with the target customers of Qingyou Space.
2. Party A is the operator of Qingyu Space, No. 398 Wensan Road, West Lake District, Hangzhou City, Zhejiang Province.

After amicable consultation and consensus between the two parties, the following agreement is signed on the matter of Party B replacing the rights and interests of Party A's space use with content promotion services, and hereby abide by it:

I. Scope of Cooperation

(1) Cooperation model

This Agreement is a replacement cooperation agreement. Party A uses the rights and interests of the green grapefruit space site to replace the content promotion service of Party B, and the two parties do not pay each other cash consideration.

(2) Party B service content

1.  During the term of cooperation, Party B shall publish no less than 4 original promotional content (including pictures or videos) related to Qingyu Space through its social account. The content shall be true, legal and positive.

2.  The content published by Party B shall clearly mark the space name and location information designated by Party A, and shall not contain false propaganda or content that violates the advertising law.

(3) Party A replacement rights and interests

1.  Party A shall provide Party B with the rights and interests of using the co-working space in the green grapefruit space. The period of use is 3 months, and the scope of use is the open workspace area designated by Party A.

2.  Party A should provide the necessary venue convenience and cooperation for Party B's content shooting.

II. Term of Cooperation

This Agreement is valid from July 1, 2026 to December 31, 2026.

III. Rights and Obligations of the Parties

(1) Rights and Obligations of Party A

1.  Party A has the right to review the content published by Party B. If the content does not meet the agreement or the requirements of laws and regulations, Party A has the right to request Party B to modify or delete it.

2.  Party A shall provide the rights and interests of space use to Party B as agreed.

3.  Party A has the right to use the cooperation content published by Party B on its own publicity channels and mark Party B's account information.

(2) Rights and Obligations of Party B

1.  Party B shall release the promotional content according to the agreed quantity, time and quality requirements.

2.  Party B shall ensure that it has the legitimate rights to the published content and does not infringe on the intellectual property rights, portrait rights and other legitimate rights and interests of any third party.

3.  During the use of Party A space, Party B shall abide by the space management regulations of Party A, take care of the space facilities, and shall not engage in illegal activities.

4.  Party B shall ensure the authenticity of its account and fan data, and shall not use false means.

IV. Intellectual Property

1.  Party B enjoys the copyright of its original published content, and Party A enjoys the right to use it for free within the scope of cooperation purposes.

2.  Party A enjoys the corresponding rights to the brand and logo of Qingyu Space. Party B can only use it with the written consent of Party A within the scope of cooperation purposes.

V. Liability for Breach

1.  If any party violates this Agreement and causes losses to the other party, it shall bear the corresponding liability for compensation in accordance with the law.

2.  If Party B fails to publish the content as agreed, Party A has the right to reduce or withdraw the rights and interests of space use accordingly; if Party A fails to provide the rights and interests of space use as agreed, Party B has the right to require Party A to supplement the corresponding rights and interests.

VI. Anti-Bribery

In entering into and performing this Agreement, both parties shall comply strictly with national laws and regulations against commercial bribery. Neither party may offer improper benefits in any form to, or accept them in any form from, the other party or any relevant third party. If either party breaches this clause, the non-breaching party may terminate this Agreement and seek compensation for its losses.

VII. Confidentiality

Both parties have the obligation to keep confidential the information not disclosed by the other party known through the signing and performance of this Agreement, and shall not disclose it to any third party without the written consent of the other party, except as otherwise provided by laws and regulations. After the termination of this Agreement, the confidentiality obligations of both parties shall not be terminated.

VIII. Force Majeure

If either party is unable to perform this Agreement due to force majeure, it shall immediately notify the other party in writing and provide relevant certificates within 15 days after the occurrence of force majeure. Both parties shall not be responsible for losses caused by force majeure.

IX. Termination of the Agreement

1.  This Agreement may be terminated in advance in the following ways: (1) The two parties terminate this Agreement in advance by mutual agreement in writing; (2) If one party breaches a serious breach of contract and has not been corrected within 15 days after written notice, the non-breaching party shall have the right to terminate this Agreement.

2.  The termination of this Agreement shall not affect the rights and obligations incurred by both parties under this Agreement before the termination date.

X. Miscellaneous Provisions

1.  This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the People's Republic of China. If there is any dispute or dispute, the two parties shall resolve it through amicable consultation; if there is no consensus, either party shall have the right to file a lawsuit in the people's court with jurisdiction over the defendant's location.

2.  This Agreement shall take effect from the date of signature (stamp) of both parties. It shall be made in duplicate, and Party A and Party B shall have one copy each, which shall have the same legal effect.

3.  Without the prior written consent of the other party, neither party shall transfer the rights and obligations under this Agreement to a third party.
