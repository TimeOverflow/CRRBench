Article 4 Rights and Obligations of Party A

1. Party A shall provide the necessary on-site access convenience and information support for Party B to carry out data collection.

2. Party A has the right to supervise and inspect the service progress and quality of Party B.

3. Party A shall pay the service fee in a timely manner as agreed in this Contract.

Article 5 Rights and Obligations of Party B

1. Party B shall carry out data collection and map production in accordance with national surveying and mapping geographic information laws and regulations and industry standards to ensure that the results are legal and accurate.

2. Party B shall properly keep the materials and commercial information provided by Party A and shall not be used for purposes other than this Contract without the written consent of Party A.

3. Party B shall be responsible for the management, safety and labor protection of the personnel stationed on site, and Party B shall be fully responsible for accidents caused by Party B personnel.

3. If Party B fails to complete the service according to the agreed schedule, the rectification and work-catching measures shall be implemented in accordance with paragraph 5 of Article 10 of this Contract.

Article 6 Confidentiality

1. Party B shall maintain strict confidentiality over Party A's trade secrets, operating data and non-public information, and over the contents of this Contract, that Party B learns in connection with entering into or performing this Contract. Party B shall not disclose any such information to a third party without Party A's written consent. Party B shall pay Party A liquidated damages of RMB 500,000.00 (in words: Five Hundred Thousand Renminbi Only) for each breach of this clause. If the liquidated damages are insufficient to cover Party A's losses, Party B shall compensate the shortfall. Party A may also terminate this Contract.

2. The confidentiality obligation stipulated in this article shall not be limited by the term of this Contract and shall remain valid for five years after the termination of this Contract.

Article 7 Liability for Breach

1. If the map results delivered by Party B do not meet the agreed standards, Party A has the right to require Party B to rework within a time limit; if it still does not meet the standard within the deadline, Party A has the right to terminate this Contract. Party B shall refund all fees collected and pay Party A liquidated damages at 30% of the total amount of this Contract.

2. If Party B completes the services late, Party B shall pay Party A liquidated damages equal to 0.2% of the total Contract amount for each day of delay. If the delay exceeds thirty days, Party A may terminate this Contract.

3. If Party B violates confidentiality obligations or data compliance requirements, resulting in Party A being subject to administrative penalties or third-party claims, Party B shall compensate Party A for all losses.

4. If Party A pays the service fee late, it shall pay liquidated damages to Party B at 0.03% of the unpaid amount for each day of delay.

5. If Party B needs to rectify or rush to work, Party B shall bear all the additional costs and ensure that the final deliverables meet the agreed standards.

Article 8 Force Majeure

If one party is unable to perform this Contract due to force majeure such as earthquakes, typhoons, floods, fires, wars, epidemics, government acts, etc., the affected party shall notify the other party in writing in a timely manner and provide proof within a reasonable period of time, and the two parties shall not be responsible for each other.

In entering into and performing this Contract, both parties shall comply strictly with national laws and regulations against commercial bribery. 1. The validity, interpretation, change, execution and dispute settlement of this Contract shall be governed by the laws of the People's Republic of China.

2. Any dispute arising out of or related to this Contract shall be resolved by amicable negotiations between the two parties; if the negotiation fails, either party shall have the right to file a lawsuit in the people's court with jurisdiction over the location of Party A.

1. This Contract takes effect on the date both parties affix their seals. It is executed in four counterparts of equal legal effect, two for each party.

3. Without the prior written consent of the other party, neither party shall assign its rights and obligations under this Contract to a third party.
