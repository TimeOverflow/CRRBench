# 2026-2027 Star Cloud Map CPS Exclusive Cooperation Project Business Contract

Party A: Xingchen New Energy Vehicle Sales Co., Ltd. (Hereinafter referred to as "Party A") Party B: CloudMap Online Technology Co., Ltd. (Hereinafter referred to as "Party B")

In view of the fact that Party A intends to carry out online promotion and pay-based (CPS) cooperation through the Party B platform, the two sides reached the following agreement on the exclusive cooperation project of CPS after amicable negotiations:

I. Scope of Cooperation

1.  Party A entrusts Party B to provide online promotion, clue collection and transaction-to-flow services for Party A's designated models on the Party B platform and its traffic entrance.

2.  This project is an exclusive cooperation. During the term of cooperation, Party A shall not carry out the same cooperation with third-party platforms on similar services. Party B enjoys the right of priority cooperation under the same conditions.

3.  For specific promotion models, promotion forms, settlement unit prices and assessment indicators, please refer to Annex III "CPS Cooperation Implementation Manual".

II. Term of Cooperation

This Agreement is valid from April 1, 2026 to March 31, 2027. Thirty days before the expiration of the term, the two parties can negotiate for renewal.

III. Settlement and Payment

1.  Settlement for the Project shall be on a cost-per-sale (CPS) basis at RMB 1,200.00 per completed vehicle sale (in words: One Thousand Two Hundred Renminbi Only).

2.  Both parties settle accounts according to the natural month. Party B shall provide Party A with the transaction details confirmed by Party A within ten working days after the end of each month. After Party A checks that it is correct, Party B will issue an invoice, and Party A shall pay within 20 working days after receiving the compliant invoice.

3.  Party B issues a special VAT invoice with an applicable tax rate of 6%. Party A's collection and Party B's invoicing information are as follows:

IV. Rights and Obligations of Party A

1.  Party A shall ensure that the information of the promoted model is true and legal, and provide the necessary brand talent and authorization.

2.  Party A shall confirm the transaction details in a timely manner and pay the service fee in accordance with this Agreement.

3.  Party A has the right to supervise the compliance of Party B's promotional content.

V. Rights and Obligations of Party B

1.  Party B shall provide promotion services for Party A according to the agreed form, location and cycle to ensure the authenticity of the promotion effect data.

2.  Party B shall ensure that its promotion content does not violate the Advertising Law and relevant laws and regulations. If Party A is punished or claimed due to the illegal promotion content of Party B, Party B shall bear full responsibility and compensate Party A for the loss.

3.  Party B shall properly keep the brand materials and commercial information provided by Party A, and shall not use them for purposes other than this project without the written consent of Party A.

3.  Party A has the right to transfer all or part of the rights and obligations under this Agreement to its affiliates without the consent of Party B; without the prior written consent of Party A, Party B shall not transfer all or part of the rights and obligations under this Agreement to a third party, otherwise Party A has the right to terminate this Agreement.

VI. Liability for Breach

1.  If Party B fails to provide promotional services or falsify promotional data as agreed, Party A has the right to request Party B to rectify within a time limit; if the circumstances are serious, Party A has the right to terminate this Agreement. Party B shall refund the corresponding service fee collected and pay Party A liquidated damages of 20% of the total amount of this Agreement.

2.  If Party B's promotional content violates the law or infringes any third party's rights or interests, and Party A incurs an administrative penalty or faces a third-party claim as a result, Party B shall compensate Party A for all resulting losses and separately pay liquidated damages of RMB 300,000.00 (in words: Three Hundred Thousand Renminbi Only).

3.  If Party A pays the service fee late, it shall pay liquidated damages to Party B at 0.03% of the unpaid amount for each day of delay.

VII. Confidentiality

Both parties shall have the obligation to keep confidential the trade secrets and undisclosed information of the other party learned through the signing and performance of this Agreement, and shall not disclose them to third parties without the written consent of the other party, except as otherwise provided by laws and regulations or required by the regulatory authorities. The obligation of this article shall not be invalidated by the cancellation or termination of this Agreement.

VIII. Force Majeure

If one party is unable to perform this Agreement due to force majeure such as earthquakes, typhoons, floods, fires, wars, epidemics, government acts, etc., the party shall notify the other party in writing in a timely manner and provide proof within a reasonable period of time, and the two parties shall not be responsible for each other.

IX. Anti-Bribery

In entering into and performing this Agreement, both parties shall comply strictly with national laws and regulations against commercial bribery. Neither party may offer improper benefits in any form to, or accept them in any form from, the other party's personnel or any relevant third party. If either party breaches this clause, the non-breaching party may terminate this Agreement and seek compensation for its losses.

X. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

1.  The validity, interpretation, change, execution and dispute settlement of this Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the People's Republic of China.

2.  Any dispute arising out of or related to this Agreement shall be resolved by amicable negotiations between the two parties; if the negotiation fails, either party has the right to file a lawsuit in the people's court with jurisdiction over the location of Party A.

XI. Miscellaneous

1.  This Agreement shall take effect from the date of seal of both parties. It shall be made in duplicate, and Party A and Party B shall have one copy each, which shall have the same legal effect.

2.  For matters not covered by this Agreement, the two parties may sign a separate written supplementary agreement.
