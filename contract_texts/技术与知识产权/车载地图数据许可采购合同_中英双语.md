2. 交付与验收 Delivery and Acceptance

3. 支付 Payment

买方应在验收合格并收到供应商开具的合规发票后三十日内支付合同总价。买方有权在供应商未按约交付时延迟付款，并不承担违约责任。
The Buyer shall pay the total contract price within thirty (30) days after the acceptance is qualified and the compliant invoice issued by the Supplier is received. The Buyer has the right to delay payment without liability if the Supplier fails to deliver as agreed.

4. 质量保证 Quality Assurance

供应商保证所交付的货物/服务符合买方技术规范及国家相关标准，质保期为验收合格后十二个月。
The Supplier warrants that the delivered goods/services conform to the Buyer's technical specifications and relevant national standards, with a warranty period of twelve (12) months after acceptance.

5. 知识产权 Intellectual Property

供应商保证其提供的货物/服务不侵犯任何第三方的知识产权。合作中产生的定制开发成果的知识产权归属由双方另行约定。
The Supplier warrants that the goods/services provided do not infringe any third party's intellectual property rights. The ownership of intellectual property in the customized development results shall be separately agreed by both parties.

6. 保密 Confidentiality

双方对因签订、履行本合同而知悉的对方商业秘密及未公开信息负有保密义务，未经对方书面同意不得向第三方披露，法律法规另有规定或监管部门要求的除外。
Each party shall keep confidential the other party's trade secrets and non-public information learned in connection with this Contract, and shall not disclose such information to any third party without the other party's written consent, except as required by laws and regulations or regulatory authorities.

7. 违约责任 Liability for Breach

任何一方违反本合同约定给对方造成损失的，应当依法承担相应的陪偿责任。供应商逾期交付的，每逾期一日应按合同总价的万分之五向买方支付违约金。
Either party that breaches this Contract and causes losses to the other party shall bear corresponding liability for compensation in accordance with the law. If the Supplier delays delivery, it shall pay the Buyer liquidated damages of 0.05% of the total contract price for each day of delay.

8. 不可抗力 Force Majeure

因不可抗力致使一方不能履行本合同的，遭遇方应及时书面通知另一方并在合理期限内提供证明，双方互不承担责任，但应采取必要措施防止损失扩大。
If a party is unable to perform this Contract due to force majeure, the affected party shall promptly notify the other party in writing and provide proof within a reasonable period, and neither party shall be liable, provided that necessary measures shall be taken to prevent the loss from expanding.

9. 反商业贿赂 Anti-Bribery

双方承诺在订立和履行本合同过程中严格遵守国家反商业贿赂法律法规，不得以任何形式向对方工作人员或相关第三方给予、收受不正当利益。
Both parties undertake to strictly comply with the national anti-commercial-bribery laws and regulations in the conclusion and performance of this Contract, and shall not, in any form, provide or accept improper benefits to/from the other party's staff or any relevant third party.

10. 法律适用及争议解决 Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

本合同之效力、解释、变更、执行及争议解决均适用中华人民共和国法律。因本合同产生的任何争议，双方应友好协商解决；协商不成的，任何一方均有权向被告所在地有管辖权的人民法院提起诉讼。
This Contract shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the People's Republic of China. Any dispute arising from this Contract shall be resolved through friendly negotiation; if no agreement is reached, either party may file a lawsuit with the people's court having jurisdiction at the place where the defendant is domiciled.

本合同经买方加盖公章后方能生效。供应商承认，在供应商签署本合同之前，已经完整阅读、充分理解并接受本合同所述/所附的条款及条件。若本合同与附件内容有冲突，以本合同为准；若各附件的内容有冲突，按附件排列的先后顺序优先适用。
