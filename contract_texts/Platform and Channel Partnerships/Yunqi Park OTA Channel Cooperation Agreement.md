[2026] Annual OTA Channel Cooperation Agreement for the [Yunqi Park] Outdoor Amusement Park

Agreement No.: 【          】

Party A: [Yunqi Cultural Tourism Development Co., Ltd. ]

Party B: [Changyou Online Travel Co., Ltd. ]

According to the relevant laws and regulations of the People's Republic of China, Party A and Party B, on the basis of equal consultation and in line with the principle of reciprocity and mutual benefit, Party A and Party B reached an agreement on matters related to the establishment of the OTA channel cooperative relationship of [Yunqi Paradise] outdoor amusement park (hereinafter referred to as "park"), and signed this Agreement to jointly abide by it.

Article 1. Method and Scope of Cooperation

1、Party A authorizes Party B to act as a non-exclusive distribution partner of the online travel channel (OTA) of amusement park tickets and related products. Party B can sell party A's designated amusement park tickets, packages and combination products online on the Party B platform.

2、Party A is responsible for the daily operation, safety and security and tourist reception of the park, and Party B is responsible for the promotion, sales and order cancellation docking of online channels.

Article 2. Term of Cooperation

This Agreement is valid from May 1, 2026 to April 30, 2027. Thirty days before the expiration of the term, the two parties can negotiate for renewal.

Article 3. Products and prices

1、Party A shall provide Party B with a list of street prices and settlement prices of amusement park products, and Party B shall sell to the outside world according to the price system confirmed by Party A.

2、Party B shall strictly implement the online street price stipulated in this Agreement, and shall not sell below the street price, and shall not affect the external sales price system of Party A channel, otherwise it shall be regarded as Party B's breach of contract; the combination products shall not be sold in split.

Article 4. Method of Settlement

1、Both parties settle accounts according to the natural month. Party B shall provide Party A with the write-off details of the previous month's sales within ten working days after the end of each month, and Party A shall issue an invoice and settle the account within fifteen working days after confirmation.

2、The settlement amount is priced in RMB. Party A collection account information:

   Party A issues a special VAT invoice, and the applicable tax rate is 6%.

Article 5. Rights and Obligations of the Parties

（1） Rights and Obligations of Party A

1、Party A shall ensure the normal operation of the park and provide amusement equipment and reception services that meet safety standards.

2、Party A shall provide Party B with accurate product information, prices and inventory in a timely manner.

（2） Rights and Obligations of Party B

1、Party B is responsible for a comprehensive and substantive review of the published content, information, etc. provided by Party A to ensure that it meets the requirements of laws and regulations and advertising release. If Party A is administratively punished or investigated for legal responsibility due to Party B's failure to properly fulfill its examination obligations, Party B shall be liable for breach of contract and compensate Party A for all losses suffered as a result.

2、Party B shall properly handle the consultation and after-sales service of Party B platform users, and assist Party A in handling complaints and disputes between Party A and ticket buyers.

3、Party A has the right to transfer all or part of the rights and obligations under this Agreement to its affiliates without the consent of Party B; without the written consent of Party A, Party B has no right to transfer all or part of the rights and obligations under this Agreement to others, otherwise, Party A has the right to terminate this Agreement.

Article 6. Liability for Breach and Termination

1、Deposit: On the date this Agreement takes effect, Party B shall pay Party A a deposit of RMB [50,000] as security for Party B's performance under this Agreement. Party A may deduct from the deposit any losses caused by Party B's failure to perform this Agreement in full and any liquidated damages payable for Party B's breach. If the deposit is insufficient during the term, Party B shall replenish it within [3] days. For each day of delay, Party B shall pay Party A liquidated damages at [1‰] of the deposit.

2、If Party A terminates this Agreement due to Party B, the deposit paid by Party B will not be returned. In addition, Party B also needs to pay Party A liquidated damages of 20% of the ticket settlement amount confirmed by both parties. If the liquidated damages are not enough to make up for the losses caused to Party A, Party A has the right to recover from Party B.

3、If Party B commits any of the following acts, Party A has the right to terminate this Agreement and require Party B to bear the liability for breach of contract: (1) Party B sells tickets below the agreed price or makes false propaganda; (2) Party B has other breaches of contract that have not been corrected within the specified period of Party A or has occurred more than [3] times.

Article 7. Confidentiality

1、The parties shall keep the contents of this Agreement confidential. Each party shall also protect any trade secrets of the other party that it obtains in performing this Agreement, refrain from disclosing them, and use them solely to perform this Agreement. Neither party may disclose them to a third party in any form or for any reason. A party that makes an unauthorized disclosure shall pay the non-breaching party RMB [200,000] in liquidated damages. If the liquidated damages do not cover the non-breaching party's losses, the breaching party shall compensate the shortfall. The non-breaching party may also terminate this Agreement.

2、The confidentiality obligations stipulated in this Agreement are still binding on both parties after the termination of this Agreement.

Article 8. Force Majeure

1、If force majeure affects the performance of the obligations of this Agreement by one party, it does not constitute a breach of contract. The party does not need to be liable, but it shall notify the other party in time and provide proof. The force majeure referred to in this Agreement refers to the objective situations that are unpredictable or unpredictable and unavoidable, such as severe natural disasters (such as typhoons, floods, earthquakes, fires and explosions), wars, rebellions, riots, government factors, etc.

Article 9. Anti-Bribery

In entering into and performing this Agreement, both parties shall comply strictly with national laws and regulations against commercial bribery. Neither party may offer improper benefits in any form to, or accept them in any form from, the other party's personnel or any relevant third party. If either party breaches this clause, the non-breaching party may terminate this Agreement and seek compensation for its losses.

Article 10. Dispute Resolution

The validity, interpretation and performance of this Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the People's Republic of China. Disputes arising from the performance of this Agreement between Party A and Party B shall be resolved through negotiation. If the negotiation fails, either party shall have the right to sue in the people's court with jurisdiction over the domicile of Party A.

Article 11. Miscellaneous

1、This Agreement shall take effect from the date of seal of both parties. It shall be made in duplicate, and Party A and Party B shall have one copy each, which shall have the same legal effect.

2、For matters not covered by this Agreement, the two parties may sign a separate written supplementary agreement.
