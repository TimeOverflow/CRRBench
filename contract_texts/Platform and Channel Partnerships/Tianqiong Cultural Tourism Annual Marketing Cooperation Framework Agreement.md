After equal and amicable consultation, Party A and B agreed on the following terms on Party A's entrustment of Party B to sell its ice and snow-themed scenic spots and polar pavilion scenic spots on the marketing, and jointly abide by them. Party A-1 provides travel big data, one-click smart tour, voice broadcasting and intelligent guidance services for Party B. Party A-2 provides tourism promotion services for Party B. Party A-1 and Party A-2 are collectively referred to as Party A, and Party A and Party B are collectively referred to as both parties. The two parties agree that this Agreement is an annual marketing agent framework agreement, which is used to agree on the marketing cooperation, settlement methods and general rights and obligations of the agency authority of the two parties during the term of cooperation.

# 1. Definitions

1.1 Tourism big data: refers to the products provided by Party A and related parties using their own technology and data advantages to provide to tourism service providers through API, so that tourism service providers can efficiently understand the operation of their tourism business places.

1.2 One-click smart tour: refers to the smart tour service provided by Party A and its affiliates for tourism service providers using their own technology and data advantages, so that its tourists can enjoy through mobile clients.

1.3 Voice broadcast: refers to the service provided by Party A and related parties using their own technology and data advantages for tourism service providers, so that users can have the opportunity to listen to the broadcast content while driving within a certain kilometer of the scenic spot under the driving navigation scenario.

# 2. Scope of Cooperation

2.1 As the marketing agent of Party A's cultural tourism products, Party B is responsible for promoting Party A's designated scenic spot tickets, packages and combination products on its channels.

2.2 Party B shall sell to the outside world in strict accordance with the price system confirmed by Party A, shall not sell below the agreed price, and shall not split and sell combination products.

2.3 The broadcast content provided by Party B customers shall not violate the Advertising Law, the Anti-Unfair Competition Law and other laws and regulations. Party A has the right to review the broadcast content and decide whether to allow it to go online.

# 3. Cooperation period and price

3.1 Validity of the contract: The two parties agreed that the term of cooperation is three years, and the price of the attached product is valid for one year. After one year, the price policy will sign a supplementary agreement or agree separately through the contact email.

3.2 The storage period of broadcast data is 30 days. If Party B needs to obtain it, it shall retrieve it from Party A in time.

# 4. Method of Settlement

4.1 Both parties settle according to the natural month. Party B shall provide Party A with the sales details of the previous month within ten working days after the end of each month, and Party A shall settle the account within fifteen working days after confirmation.

# 5. Authorized materials

5.1 Party B shall provide Party A with basic information of scenic spots, hotels and other travel-related places within the time limit determined by both parties, including pictures, text, audio and video, documents and other contents (hereinafter referred to as "authorized materials") for the display and publicity of cultural tourism products.

5.2 Party A and its affiliates have the legal right to use the aforementioned authorized materials, and may make non-substantial modifications and reasonable use of the authorized materials of the scenic spot according to their own business adjustments.

# 6. Brand use

6.1 During the validity period of this Agreement, one party to this Agreement may use the other party's trademark, logo and other product or service logo as well as the enterprise name and logo for the purpose of fulfilling this Agreement after obtaining the written authorization of the other party.

6.2 If Party B needs to use Tianqiong's trademark, logo and other brand elements, it shall sign a written brand authorization agreement in accordance with the Tianqiong brand use authorization regulations. Before the visual design and materials can be used online, it can only be used after obtaining the written or email consent of Tianqiong. Both parties shall ensure that such use is limited to the cooperation matters, brand scope and the name of the authorized person under this Agreement, and cannot be sub-authorized without permission. If this Agreement is terminated, the above authorization will be automatically terminated.

# 7. Confidentiality

7.1 "Confidential information" under this Agreement refers to any non-public commercial information related to the business, finance, technology and other aspects of the disclosing party provided by one party (hereinafter referred to as the "disclosing party") to the other party (referred to as the "receiving party") in the process of evaluation, consultation, negotiation and cooperation to achieve the purpose of cooperation in this Agreement.

7.2 The receiving party shall keep the confidential information confidential and shall not disclose it to a third party without the written consent of the disclosing party. It shall only be used for the purpose of fulfilling this Agreement.

7.3 The confidentiality obligations stipulated in this article are not limited by the term of this Agreement and are still valid after the termination of this Agreement.

# 8. Liability for Breach

8.1 If any party violates this Agreement and causes losses to the other party, it shall bear the corresponding liability for compensation in accordance with the law.

8.2 If one party delays the performance of its settlement obligations, it shall pay liquidated damages to the other party at 0.05% of the amount payable for each day of delay.

# 9. Term and Termination

9.1 Upon the parties affixing their official company seals or designated contract seals, this Agreement shall take effect on [2026]/[3]/[127] and remain in force through [2027]/[3]/[31].

9.2 Thirty days before the expiration of this Agreement, the two parties may negotiate the renewal of the contract.

# 10. Force Majeure

If one party is unable to perform this Agreement due to force majeure such as earthquakes, typhoons, floods, fires, wars, epidemics, government acts, etc., the party shall notify the other party in writing in a timely manner and provide proof within a reasonable period of time, and the two parties shall not be responsible for each other.

# 11. Anti-Bribery

In entering into and performing this Agreement, both parties shall comply strictly with national laws and regulations against commercial bribery. Neither party may offer improper benefits in any form to, or accept them in any form from, the other party's personnel or any relevant third party. If either party breaches this clause, the non-breaching party may terminate this Agreement and seek compensation for its losses.

# 12. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

12.1 The validity, interpretation, change, execution and dispute settlement of this Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the People's Republic of China.

12.2 Any dispute arising out of or related to this Agreement shall be resolved by amicable negotiations between the two parties; if the negotiation fails, either party shall have the right to file a lawsuit in the people's court with jurisdiction in the location of Party A-1.

# 13. Miscellaneous

13.1 For matters not covered by this Agreement, the two parties may sign a separate written supplementary agreement, and the supplementary agreement shall have the same legal effect as this Agreement.

13.2 Without the prior written consent of the other party, neither party shall transfer the rights and obligations under this Agreement to a third party.

13.3 This Agreement is executed in four counterparts of equal legal effect. Party A-1, Party A-2 and Party B shall each retain one counterpart, and the remaining counterpart shall be kept on file.
