In view of the agreement between Party A and Party B on the joint operation of the "daily special offer" business position in the Party B client, and agreed to jointly bear the cost and distribute the benefits in the process, the two parties reached the following agreement in accordance with the principles of equality, voluntariness, honesty and credit:

I. Scope of Cooperation

1. Party A and Party B jointly operate the "daily special offer" business position (hereinafter referred to as the "cooperation position") in the Party B client. Party A is responsible for commodity selection, marketing activity planning and merchant docking, and Party B is responsible for traffic entrance, technical support and payment settlement.

2. During the term of cooperation, the two parties shall jointly bear the operating costs of the cooperation position in accordance with the proportion agreed in this Agreement, and distribute the income generated by the cooperation position.

II. Term of Cooperation

This Agreement is valid from April 1, 2026 to March 31, 2027. Thirty days before the expiration of the term, the two parties can negotiate the renewal of the contract.

III. Cost and benefit distribution

1. The operating costs of the cooperative position (including but not limited to marketing subsidies, technical maintenance and customer service expenses) shall be jointly borne by Party A and Party B according to the ratio of 50%:50%.

2. The net income generated by the cooperative position (that is, the balance after deducting the cost of income) shall be distributed by Party A and Party B according to the ratio of 60%:40%, of which Party A accounts for 60% and Party B accounts for 40%.

3. Both parties shall complete the accounting of the costs and benefits of the previous month within 15 working days after the end of each month, and issue a settlement form. If one party does not raise a written objection within ten working days after receiving the settlement form, it shall be deemed to be confirmed.

IV. Settlement and Payment

1. The settlement amount is calculated and paid in RMB.

V. Rights and Obligations of the Parties

1. Party A shall ensure that the goods and marketing content provided are legal and compliant and do not infringe on the legitimate rights and interests of any third party.

2. Party B should ensure the stable operation of the technology of the cooperative position and provide necessary traffic support.

3. Neither party shall carry out external activities or make commitments in the name of the other party without authorization.

VI. Confidentiality

Both parties have the obligation to keep confidential the trade secrets, operating data and undisclosed information of the other party learned through the signing and performance of this Agreement, and shall not disclose them to any third party without the written consent of the other party, except as otherwise provided by laws and regulations or required by the regulatory authorities. The obligation of this article shall not be invalidated by the cancellation or termination of this Agreement.

VII. Liability for Breach

1. If any party violates this Agreement and causes losses to the other party, it shall bear the corresponding liability for compensation in accordance with the law.

2. If one party delays the performance of its settlement obligations, it shall pay liquidated damages to the other party at 0.05% of the outstanding amount for each day of delay.

VIII. Anti-Bribery

In entering into and performing this Agreement, both parties shall comply strictly with national laws and regulations against commercial bribery. Neither party may offer kickbacks, cash gifts or other improper benefits in any form to, or accept them in any form from, the other party's personnel or any relevant third party. If either party breaches this clause, the non-breaching party may terminate this Agreement and seek compensation for its losses.

IX. Force Majeure

If one party is unable to perform this Agreement due to force majeure such as earthquakes, typhoons, floods, fires, wars, epidemics, government acts, etc., the party shall notify the other party in writing in a timely manner and provide proof within a reasonable period of time, and the two parties shall not be responsible for each other.

X. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

1. The validity, interpretation, change, execution and dispute settlement of this Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the People's Republic of China.

2. Any dispute arising out of or related to this Agreement shall be resolved by amicable negotiations between the two parties; if the negotiation fails, either party shall have the right to file a lawsuit in the people's court with jurisdiction over the defendant's location.

XI. Effectiveness and Miscellaneous Provisions

1. This Agreement shall take effect from the date of signature and stamped by Party A and Party B. It shall be made in duplicate, one copy for each Party A and Party B, and shall have the same legal effect.

2. For matters not covered by this Agreement, the two parties may sign a written supplementary agreement separately, and the supplementary agreement shall have the same legal effect as this Agreement.

3. Without the prior written consent of the other party, neither party shall transfer the rights and obligations under this Agreement to a third party.
