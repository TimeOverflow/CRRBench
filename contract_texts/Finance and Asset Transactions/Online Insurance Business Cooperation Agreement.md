Online Insurance Business Cooperation Agreement

(Platform version of distribution insurance, platform version of meal damage insurance, platform version of food safety insurance)

Party A-1: Jingyue Network Technology (Shanghai) Co., Ltd.

Party A-2: Shanghai Jingyue Information Technology Co., Ltd.

Party B: Anping Online Property Insurance Co., Ltd.

Party A-1 and Party A-2 are collectively referred to as Party A. After amicable negotiations, Party A and Party B reached the following cooperation agreement on Party A platform merchants and distribution personnel to insure Party B Internet insurance products:

Article 1 Scope of Cooperation

1. Party B provides Internet insurance products such as distribution insurance, food damage insurance, food safety insurance, etc. for Party A platform merchants and distribution personnel. Party A provides insurance scenarios and technical service support for Party B.

2. For specific insurance product plans, insurance liabilities, rates and insurance processes, please refer to Annex II Insurance Terms.

3. The two parties shall cooperate in accordance with the regulations on the supervision of Internet insurance business to ensure business compliance and data integrity.

Article 2 Term of Cooperation

This Agreement is valid from July 1, 2026 to June 30, 2027. Thirty days before the expiration of the term, the two parties can negotiate for renewal.

Article 3 Insurance premium and settlement

1. The premium shall be calculated according to the standard agreed in Annex I "Rate and Settlement Standards" and settled according to the natural month.

2. Party B shall provide Party A with the insurance and underwriting details of the previous month within 15 working days after the end of each month. After Party A checks that there is no mistake, the two parties will settle the account as agreed.

3. The settlement amount is priced in RMB. Party B issues a special VAT invoice with an applicable tax rate of 6%.

Article 4 Rights and Obligations of Party A

1. Party A shall provide Party B with technical integration for insurance applications and ensure that the application data is true, accurate and complete.

2. Party A shall assist Party B to complete the preliminary collection of emergency collection, transmission and claim settlement materials of insurance information.

3. Party A shall abide by the regulations on the supervision of Internet insurance business, and shall not illegally collect premiums, falsely publicize or mislead policyholders.

Article 5 Rights and Obligations of Party B

1. Party B shall have legal Internet insurance business qualifications and bear insurance liability in accordance with the insurance terms agreed in Annex II's Underwriting Plan.

2. Party B shall complete the underwriting, claim settlement and preservation services in a timely and compliant manner to protect the legitimate rights and interests of the policyholder and the insured.

2. Party B shall have the obligation to keep the data and commercial information provided by Party A confidential and shall not be used for purposes other than this Agreement without the written consent of Party A.

Article 6 Data Compliance and Confidentiality

1. Both parties shall abide by the relevant laws and regulations on data security and personal information protection, legally collect, use and transmit relevant personal information, and obtain the necessary authorization from the relevant subjects.

2. Both parties shall have the obligation to keep confidential the trade secrets and undisclosed information of the other party learned through the signing and performance of this Agreement, and shall not disclose them to third parties without the written consent of the other party, except as otherwise provided by laws and regulations or required by the regulatory authorities.

Article 7 Liability for Breach

1. If Party A violates data compliance requirements, illegally collects premiums or provides false insurance data, resulting in Party B to suffer regulatory penalties or losses, Party A shall pay Party B liquidated damages of 30% of the total annual premium of this Agreement and compensate Party B for all losses.

2. If Party A fails to provide the technical integration or application data as agreed, and that failure affects Party B's underwriting, Party A shall pay Party B liquidated damages equal to 0.2% of the premiums for the relevant month for each day of delay.

3. If Party B fails to provide coverage as agreed or delays claims handling, Party B shall remedy the failure within the specified period. If Party B pays a claim late, Party B shall pay Party A liquidated damages equal to 0.02% of the claim amount due for each day of delay.

Article 8 Force Majeure

If one party is unable to perform this Agreement due to force majeure such as earthquakes, typhoons, floods, fires, wars, epidemics, government actions or major adjustments in financial supervision policies, the encounter party shall notify the other party in writing in time and provide proof within a reasonable period of time, and the two parties shall not be responsible for each other.

Article 9 Anti-Bribery

In entering into and performing this Agreement, both parties shall comply strictly with national laws and regulations against commercial bribery. Neither party may offer improper benefits in any form to, or accept them in any form from, the other party's personnel or any relevant third party. If either party breaches this clause, the non-breaching party may terminate this Agreement and seek compensation for its losses.

Article 10 Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

1. The validity, interpretation, change, execution and dispute settlement of this Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the People's Republic of China.

2. Any dispute arising out of or related to this Agreement shall be resolved by amicable negotiations between the two parties; if the negotiation fails, either party shall have the right to file a lawsuit in the people's court with jurisdiction where Party A-1 is located.

Article 11 Effectiveness and Miscellaneous Provisions

1. This Agreement takes effect on the date both parties affix their seals. It is executed in six counterparts of equal legal effect. Party A-1, Party A-2 and Party B shall each retain two counterparts.

2. For matters not covered by this Agreement, the two parties may sign a separate written supplementary agreement.

3. Without the prior written consent of the other party, neither party shall transfer the rights and obligations under this Agreement to a third party.
