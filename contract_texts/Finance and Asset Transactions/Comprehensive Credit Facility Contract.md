1.  Party B grants Party A a comprehensive credit facility of RMB 50,000,000.00 (in words: Fifty Million Renminbi Only). The credit facility may be used for working capital loans, bank acceptance drafts, letters of credit and other banking products.

Article 2 Withdrawal and loan

1.  When Party A uses the credit limit, it shall submit the "Withdrawal Application Approval Form" (Annex II) and relevant materials to Party B, and the loan shall be approved by Party B.

2.  If Party B fails to lend money in full and in a timely manner as agreed and causes losses to Party A, Party A shall be compensated for the direct losses suffered as a result.

3.  If any party violates the agreement of this Contract and causes losses to the other party, it shall bear the corresponding liability for compensation in accordance with the law.

Article 9 Force Majeure

If one party is unable to perform this Contract due to force majeure such as earthquakes, typhoons, floods, fires, wars, epidemics, government actions or major adjustments to financial policies, the affected party shall notify the other party in writing in a timely manner and provide proof within a reasonable period of time. Both parties shall not be liable to each other, but shall take necessary measures to prevent the expansion of losses.

Article 10 Anti-Bribery

In entering into and performing this Contract, both parties shall comply strictly with national laws and regulations against commercial bribery. Neither party may offer improper benefits in any form to, or accept them in any form from, the other party's personnel or any relevant third party. If either party breaches this clause, the non-breaching party may terminate this Contract and seek compensation for its losses.

1.  The validity, interpretation, change and execution of this Contract shall be governed by the laws of the People's Republic of China.

2.  Any dispute arising out of this Contract or related to this Contract shall be resolved by amicable negotiations between the two parties; if the negotiation fails, it may apply to the arbitration committee with jurisdiction for arbitration or file a lawsuit with the people's court.
