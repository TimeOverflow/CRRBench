# GUARANTY

THIS GUARANTY, dated as of the ___ day of ___, 2026, made by Nebula Group Services Limited, having an address 26/F, Tower One, Times Square, 1 Matheson Street, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong ("Guarantor") in favor of Meridian Austin Eastgate 3 LLC, a Delaware limited liability company, having an address c/o 3343 Peachtree Rd NE, Suite 145 PMB 3021, Atlanta GA 30326 ("Landlord").

# WITNESSETH:

WHEREAS, simultaneously herewith, the Landlord has entered into a Data Center Lease (the "Lease"), dated as of the date hereof with Nebula Cloud LLC, a California limited liability company, having its principal place of business at 525 Alamanor Ave., 4th Floor, Sunnyvale, CA 94085 ("Tenant");

WHEREAS, the Landlord would not have entered into the Lease unless Guarantor executed and delivered this Guaranty.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the execution and delivery of the Lease by the Landlord, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and legal sufficiency of which Guarantor hereby acknowledges, Guarantor hereby agrees as follows:

1. GUARANTEE OF OBLIGATIONS. Guarantor hereby absolutely and unconditionally guarantees to the Landlord the full and punctual payment and performance of all obligations of the Tenant under the Lease, including without limitation the payment of rent and other charges when due (the "Guaranteed Obligations"). The maximum aggregate amount guaranteed hereunder shall not exceed US $8,000,000 (Say: US Dollars Eight Million Only).

2. NATURE OF GUARANTY. This Guaranty is a guaranty of payment and performance and not merely of collection. The Surety shall be primarily and directly liable to the Landlord for the Guaranteed Obligations, and the Landlord shall not be required to proceed first against the Tenant or against any other security before enforcing this Guaranty.

3. WAIVER OF DEFENSES. To the fullest extent permitted by law, Guarantor waives any right to require the Landlord to proceed first against the Tenant, to proceed against any collateral, or to exhaust any other remedy before enforcing this Guaranty. The obligations of the Surety hereunder shall not be affected by any amendment, waiver, or consent granted in respect of the Lease.

4. CONTINUING GUARANTY. This Guaranty shall be a continuing guaranty and shall remain in full force and effect until the earlier of (a) the expiration or earlier termination of the Lease and the full payment and performance of the Guaranteed Obligations, and (b) the written release of this Guaranty by the Landlord.

5. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES. Guarantor represents and warrants that: (a) it is a corporation duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of Hong Kong; (b) it has full power and authority to execute and deliver this Guaranty; and (c) this Guaranty constitutes its legal, valid and binding obligation, enforceable in accordance with its terms.

6. ASSIGNMENT. Neither party may assign this Guaranty without the prior written consent of the other party, except that the Landlord may assign this Guaranty to any transferee of the Landlord's interest in the Lease and Guarantor may assign this Guaranty to an affiliate without such consent.

7. GOVERNING LAW. This Guaranty shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, United States of America, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

8. DISPUTE RESOLUTION. Any dispute, controversy or claim arising out of or relating to this Guaranty shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of the State of Georgia, United States of America, and Guarantor irrevocably consents and submits to the personal jurisdiction and venue of such courts and waives any objection thereto.

9. NOTICES. All notices under this Guaranty shall be in writing and delivered to the addresses set forth above, or to such other address as either party may designate by written notice.

10. ENTIRE AGREEMENT. This Guaranty constitutes the entire agreement between the Landlord and Guarantor with respect to the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior agreements. This Guaranty may only be amended by a written instrument signed by both parties.
