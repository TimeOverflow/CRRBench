RMB Deposit Agreement of the Central Plains Bank Unit

Agreement No.: ZYYH-XD-2026-0188

Party A: Zhejiang Xingchen Technology Co., Ltd.

Party B: Zhongyuan Bank Co., Ltd. Hangzhou Branch

According to the relevant provisions of the People's Bank of China, Party A and Party B reached the following agreement on handling the RMB agreement deposit arrangement of the unit after amicable consultation:

I. Agreement deposit account and amount

1.  Party A opened a RMB settlement account in Party B, account name: Zhejiang Xingchen Technology Co., Ltd., account number: 73350166889052500888, account opening bank: Zhongyuan Bank Co., Ltd. Hangzhou branch.

2.  The parties agree that the retained balance threshold for Party A's agreement deposit account identified above is RMB 500,000.00 (in words: Five Hundred Thousand Renminbi Only). The balance above this threshold accrues interest at the agreement deposit rate. The portion of the balance up to and including the threshold accrues interest at the demand deposit rate.

3.  The interest rate of the agreement deposit shall be subject to the interest rate policy announced by the People's Bank of China and Party B. If the interest rate is adjusted, the adjusted interest rate shall be implemented.

II. Interest calculation and interest settlement

1.  The interest on the agreement deposit is calculated on a daily basis and the interest is settled quarterly. Party B shall pay Party A the interest of the previous interest cycle on the 20th of the last month of each quarter.

2.  The interest calculation is subject to the actual balance of Party A account and the corresponding interest rate. The interest calculation formula is: interest = cumulative interest accumulation × daily interest rate.

3.  Party A has the right to inquire about the account balance, interest accrual and settlement, and Party B shall provide reconciliation services for Party A.

III. Rights and Obligations of Party A

1.  Party A shall ensure that the source of funds deposited is legal and abide by the relevant regulations of the People's Bank of China on account management and anti-money laundering.

2.  Party A has the right to withdraw account funds as agreed, and Party B shall guarantee the freedom of access to Party A's funds.

3.  If there is any change in the account information of Party A, Party B shall be notified in writing in time.

IV. Rights and Obligations of Party B

1.  Party B shall provide Party A with safe and convenient agreement deposit services to ensure the safety of Party A's account funds.

2.  Party B shall pay interest to Party A according to the agreed interest rate and interest calculation method.

3.  Party B has the obligation to keep Party A's account information and deposits confidential, and shall not disclose it to any third party except as otherwise stipulated by laws and regulations or requested by the competent authorities according to law.

V. Confidentiality

Both parties have the obligation to keep confidential the other party's trade secrets, account information and undisclosed information learned through the signing and performance of this Agreement, and shall not disclose them to third parties without the written consent of the other party, except as otherwise provided by laws and regulations or required by the regulatory authorities. After the termination of this Agreement, the confidentiality obligations of both parties shall not be terminated.

VI. Liability for Breach

1.  If any party violates this Agreement and causes losses to the other party, it shall bear the corresponding liability for compensation in accordance with the law.

2.  If Party B fails to pay interest as agreed or deducts Party A's funds without authorization, it shall be corrected and Party A shall be compensated for the corresponding losses.

3.  If Party A violates the account management and anti-money laundering regulations and causes losses to Party B, it shall bear the corresponding liability.

VII. Force Majeure

If one party is unable to perform this Agreement due to force majeure such as earthquakes, typhoons, floods, fires, wars, epidemics, government actions or major failures of the financial system, the receiving party shall notify the other party in writing in a timely manner and provide proof within a reasonable period of time. The two parties shall not be responsible for each other, but shall take necessary measures to prevent the expansion of losses.

VIII. Anti-Bribery

In entering into and performing this Agreement, both parties shall comply strictly with national laws and regulations against commercial bribery. Neither party may offer improper benefits in any form to, or accept them in any form from, the other party's personnel or any relevant third party. If either party breaches this clause, the non-breaching party may terminate this Agreement and seek compensation for its losses.

IX. Term and Termination

1.  This Agreement shall take effect from the date of signature and seal of both parties and shall be valid for one year. If neither party raises any objection before the expiration of the period, this Agreement shall be automatically extended for one year.

2.  If Party A terminates the agreement deposit arrangement, it shall notify Party B in writing in advance, and Party B shall handle the account change procedures for Party A after settling the interest.

3.  The cancellation or termination of this Agreement shall not affect the rights and obligations incurred by both parties under this Agreement before the termination date.

X. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

1.  The validity, interpretation, change, execution and dispute settlement of this Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the People's Republic of China.

2.  Any dispute arising out of or related to this Agreement shall be resolved by amicable negotiations between the two parties; if the negotiation fails, either party has the right to file a lawsuit in the people's court with jurisdiction over the location of Party A.

XI. Miscellaneous

1.  For matters not covered by this Agreement, the two parties may sign a written supplementary agreement separately, and the supplementary agreement shall have the same legal effect as this Agreement.

2.  Without the prior written consent of the other party, neither party shall transfer the rights and obligations under this Agreement to a third party.

3.  This Agreement shall be made in duplicate, and Party A and Party B shall each hold one copy, which shall have the same legal effect.
