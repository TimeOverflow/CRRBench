Commercial Air Purification Equipment Lease Agreement

Lessor: Zeyun Network Technology (Shenzhen) Co., Ltd. (Party A) Tenant: Lanpo Technology (Shenzhen) Co., Ltd. (Party B)

Signing Location: Unit 1003, Building T2, Zeyun Building, South District, Science and Technology Park, Nanshan District, Shenzhen City Signing Time: May 1, 2026

In accordance with the Civil Code of the People's Republic of China and relevant provisions, this Contract is hereby signed by amicable consultation and consensus between the two parties.

I. List of equipment

1. Party A leases commercial air purification equipment to Party B. For details of the name, model, quantity and unit price of the equipment, please refer to Annex I of this Contract "List of Rental Equipment".

2. The ownership of the equipment belongs to Party A, and Party B enjoys the right to use the equipment during the lease period.

II. Lease term

1. The lease term is from June 1, 2026 to May 31, 2028, a total of 24 months.

2. At the expiration of the lease period, if Party B needs to renew the lease, it shall submit a written application to Party A 30 days before the expiration of the lease period, and the two parties shall sign a separate lease renewal contract by consensus.

III. Rent and payment

1. Equipment rent is RMB 36,000.00 per month (in words: Thirty-Six Thousand Renminbi Only). The aggregate rent for the Lease Term is RMB 864,000.00 (in words: Eight Hundred Sixty-Four Thousand Renminbi Only).

2. Party B shall pay the rent quarterly and pay the quarterly rent to Party A before the 10th of the first month of each quarter.

3. Party A issues a special VAT invoice, and the applicable tax rate is 13%. Party A collection account information:
   Account Name: Zeyun Network Technology (Shenzhen) Co., Ltd.
   Account Bank: China Merchants Bank Co., Ltd. Shenzhen Science and Technology Park Branch
   Bank Account No.: 755917175410301

IV. Equipment Delivery and Acceptance

1. Party A shall deliver the equipment to the designated location of Party B before June 1, 2026, and Party B shall complete the acceptance within seven working days after receiving the equipment.

2. After acceptance, both parties shall sign the Equipment Handover Form (Annex II), which is an integral part of this Contract.

V. Equipment use and maintenance

1. Party B shall use the equipment reasonably in accordance with the instructions for use of the equipment and the requirements of Party A, and shall not disassemble, modify or transfer the equipment without authorization.

2. During the lease period, Party A shall be responsible for the daily maintenance, maintenance and repair of non-man-man-made damage of the equipment; if the equipment is damaged due to improper use by Party B, Party B shall be responsible for repair or bear the repair cost.

3. Party A shall inspect the equipment regularly to ensure the normal operation of the equipment.

VI. Rights and Obligations of the Parties

1. Party A shall guarantee that it has the legal ownership or disposal right of the rental equipment, and there is no ownership dispute over the equipment.

2. Party B shall pay the rent on time, keep and use the equipment properly, and abide by the relevant safety regulations.

3. Without the written consent of Party A, Party B shall not sublet, lend or use the equipment for illegal activities.

VII. Confidentiality

Both parties have the obligation to keep confidential the other party's trade secrets and undisclosed information learned through the signing and performance of this Contract, and shall not disclose them to third parties without the written consent of the other party, except as otherwise provided by laws and regulations. After the termination of this Contract, the confidentiality obligations of both parties shall not be terminated.

VIII. Liability for Breach

1. If any party violates the agreement of this Contract and causes losses to the other party, it shall bear the corresponding liability for compensation in accordance with the law.

2. If Party B pays the rent late, it shall pay liquidated damages to Party A at 0.05% of the unpaid amount for each day of delay; if it is overdue for more than 30 days, Party A has the right to terminate this Contract and take back the equipment.

3. If Party A fails to deliver the equipment as agreed or fails to fulfill the maintenance obligations, causing Party B to be unable to use it normally, the corresponding rent shall be reduced or waived according to the actual impact period.

IX. Force Majeure

If one party is unable to perform this Contract due to force majeure such as earthquakes, typhoons, floods, fires, wars, epidemics, government acts, etc., the affected party shall notify the other party in writing in a timely manner and provide proof within a reasonable period of time, and the two parties shall not be responsible for each other.

X. Anti-Bribery

In entering into and performing this Contract, both parties shall comply strictly with national laws and regulations against commercial bribery. Neither party may offer improper benefits in any form to, or accept them in any form from, the other party's personnel or any relevant third party. If either party breaches this clause, the non-breaching party may terminate this Contract and seek compensation for its losses.

XI. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

1. The validity, interpretation, change, execution and dispute settlement of this Contract shall be governed by the laws of the People's Republic of China.

2. Any dispute arising out of or related to this Contract shall be resolved by amicable negotiations between the two parties; if the negotiation fails, either party shall have the right to file a lawsuit in the people's court with jurisdiction over the location of Party A.

XII. Effectiveness and Miscellaneous Provisions

1. This Contract shall take effect from the date of the seal of both parties. It shall be made in duplicate, and Party A and Party B shall hold one copy each, which shall have the same legal effect.

2. For matters not covered by this Contract, the two parties may sign a written supplementary agreement separately, which shall have the same legal effect as this Contract.

3. Without the prior written consent of the other party, neither party shall assign its rights and obligations under this Contract to a third party.
