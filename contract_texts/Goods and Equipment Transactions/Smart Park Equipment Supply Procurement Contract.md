# Procurement contract

Party A (Buyer): [Ruilin Intelligent Technology Co., Ltd. ]

Party B (Seller): [Chenghui Space Technology Services (Hangzhou) Co., Ltd. ]

Contract signing date: 2026-06-18 Signing place: [Yuhang] District, [Hangzhou] City.

According to the Civil Code of the People's Republic of China and relevant laws and regulations, Party A and Party B reached the following agreement on Party A's purchase of smart equipment from Party B through equal consultation:

Article 1 Subject of Procurement

1. Party B supplies smart park management equipment to Party A, including intelligent access control terminals, environmental sensors and edge computing gateways, etc. For specific equipment names, models, quantities and unit prices, please refer to Annex I "List of Supply Equipment".

2. For the technical parameters, performance indicators and acceptance standards of the equipment, please refer to Annex II Technical Specifications.

3. Party B shall ensure that the equipment supplied is a brand-new, unused and qualified product that meets national and industry standards.

Article 2 Contract Price

1. The total Contract price is RMB 1,280,000.00 (in words: One Million Two Hundred Eighty Thousand Renminbi Only), inclusive of tax and all costs, including equipment, transportation, installation, commissioning, and taxes and charges.

2. Payment progress: 30% will be paid after the contract is signed and taken effect, 60% will be paid after the equipment is accepted, and 10% will be paid after the warranty period expires and there is no quality problem.

Article 3 Delivery and Acceptance

1. Party B shall deliver all equipment to the designated location of Party A before September 30, 2026, and complete the installation and commissioning.

2. Party A shall organize the acceptance within 15 working days after receiving the equipment, and the acceptance process shall be implemented in accordance with paragraph 3 of Article 9 of this Contract.

3. Party B should provide a complete product qualification certificate, technical documents and operation manual.

Article 4 Invoicing and Settlement

1. Party B shall issue a special VAT invoice of the corresponding amount to Party A before Party A pays each payment, and the applicable tax rate is 13%.

2. Party B collection account information:
   Account Name: Chenghui Space Technology Services (Hangzhou) Co., Ltd.
   Account Bank: Hangzhou Yuhang Branch of Industrial and Commercial Bank of China Co., Ltd.
   Bank Account No.: 1202022309900188876

Article 5 Rights and Obligations of Party A

1. Party A shall pay the contract price in a timely manner as agreed in this Contract.

2. Party A has the right to supervise and inspect the supply progress and equipment quality of Party B.

3. Party A shall provide the necessary on-site conditions for the installation and commissioning of Party B.

Article 6 Rights and Obligations of Party B

1. Party B shall supply according to the agreed time, quantity and quality standards (see Annex II Technical Parameters of Equipment for details) and ensure the stable and reliable performance of the equipment.

2. Party B shall be responsible for the installation, commissioning and operation training of Party A personnel.

3. Party B shall provide a warranty service of not less than 24 months, and Party B shall be responsible for free repair or replacement of failures caused by equipment quality problems during the warranty period.

3. Without the prior written consent of Party A, Party B shall not transfer all or part of its rights and obligations under this Contract to a third party.

Article 7 Liability for Breach

1. If Party B delivers the equipment late, Party B shall pay Party A liquidated damages equal to 0.1% of the total Contract price for each day of delay. If the delay exceeds thirty days, Party A may terminate this Contract, and Party B shall refund all amounts received and compensate Party A for its losses.

2. If the equipment delivered by Party B does not meet the agreed quality standards and still does not meet the standard after two replacements, Party A has the right to terminate this Contract, and Party B shall pay Party A liquidated damages of 20% of the total price of this Contract.

3. If Party B fails to provide quality assurance services as agreed, Party A has the right to entrust a third party to repair, and the cost shall be borne by Party B, and Party B shall pay Party A liquidated damages of RMB 50,000.00 each time.

4. If Party A pays the contract price late, it shall pay Party B liquidated damages of 0.03% of the outstanding amount for each day of delay.

Article 8 Confidentiality

Both parties have the obligation to keep confidential the trade secrets, technical information and undisclosed information of the other party learned from the signing and performance of this Contract, and shall not disclose them to third parties without the written consent of the other party, except as otherwise provided by laws and regulations or required by the regulatory authorities. The obligation of this article shall not be invalidated by the cancellation or termination of this Contract.

Article 9 Anti-Bribery

In entering into and performing this Contract, both parties shall comply strictly with national laws and regulations against commercial bribery. Neither party may offer improper benefits in any form to, or accept them in any form from, the other party's personnel or any relevant third party. If either party breaches this clause, the non-breaching party may terminate this Contract and seek compensation for its losses.

Article 10 Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

1. The validity, interpretation, change, execution and dispute settlement of this Contract shall be governed by the laws of the People's Republic of China.

2. Any dispute arising out of this Contract or related to this Contract shall be resolved by amicable negotiations between the two parties; if the negotiation fails, either party shall have the right to file a lawsuit in the people's court with jurisdiction over the defendant's location.

Article 11 Effectiveness and Miscellaneous Provisions

1. This Contract takes effect on the date both parties affix their seals. It is executed in four counterparts of equal legal effect, two for each party.

2. For matters not covered by this Contract, the two parties may sign a written supplementary agreement separately, which shall have the same legal effect as this Contract.
