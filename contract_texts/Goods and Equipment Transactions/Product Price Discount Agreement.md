# Discount Agreement

Date:  April 18, 2026

Following negotiations between Huachen Automobile Manufacturing Co., Ltd. (“Party A”) and CloudMap Software Co., Ltd. (“Party B”), Party B agrees to grant Party A an aggregate price discount of RMB 600,000.00, exclusive of VAT, on the products listed below that Party B supplied to Party A from January 1, 2026 through March 31, 2026. This Agreement may serve as notice to the finance departments of both parties to complete the relevant procedures.

Discount Details:

| No. | Part No. | Part Name | Discount Amount (RMB) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | CM-NAV-2026 | In-vehicle navigation map data | 380,000.00 |
| 2 | CM-POI-2026 | POI data update service | 220,000.00 |

Total: 600,000.00 RMB (exclusive of VAT)

1. This Discount Agreement applies to the price of the products supplied by Party B to Party A during the period stated above. The discount excludes VAT, and Party A shall reduce its corresponding accounts payable by the discount amount.

2. Within fifteen (15) working days after this Discount Agreement is signed, Party B shall process the relevant accounting offsets for the discount amount and cooperate with Party A in completing the accounting adjustments.

3. This Discount Agreement is executed in two originals, one for each party. It shall take effect upon affixation of both parties’ company seals and serve as the basis for their finance departments to complete the relevant procedures.

Party A (Seal): Huachen Automobile Manufacturing Co., Ltd.

Party B (Seal): CloudMap Software Co., Ltd.
