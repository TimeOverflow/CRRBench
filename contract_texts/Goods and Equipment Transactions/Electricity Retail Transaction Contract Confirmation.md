# Retail Transaction Contract Confirmation

Party A (power purchaser, electricity user): CloudMap Network Technology Co., Ltd.
Party B (Electricity Sales Party, Electricity Sales Company): Huaneng Zhejiang Integrated Energy Services Co., Ltd.

Before participating in the transaction, Parties A and B, in accordance with the principle of integrity and voluntariness, hereby solemnly declare that the two parties have fully understood the laws, regulations and policies on electricity transactions, carefully studied the "Implementation Rules for Medium and Long-term Transactions of Zhejiang Power (3.0 Version)", the "Implementation Rules of Zhejiang Power Retail Market (3.0 Version)" and other documents, as well as the "Contract for the Purchase and Sale of Electricity between Zhejiang Electricity Sales Company and Power Users (Model Text, 2026 Edition)" published on the trading platform. Voluntarily choose to sign a electricity purchase and sale contract between electricity sales companies and power users in 2026 in the form of Retail Transaction Contract Confirmation. Parties A and B accept the above relevant documents as the basis for the transaction. The terms and contents of the retail transaction contract shall be subject to the model text. They accept the rights and obligations of both parties stipulated in the model text, and confirm the relevant contents as follows:

I. Basic information of the transaction

1.  The electricity account number, electricity address and voltage level of Party A shall be subject to the registration information of the trading platform.

2.  The electricity seller (Party B) provides electricity retail services for Party A under this Confirmation from January 1, 2026 to December 31, 2026.

3.  Both parties confirm that the electricity, electricity price and settlement method of this retail transaction shall be implemented in accordance with the model text and the rules announced by the trading platform.

II. Transaction price and settlement

1.  The retail electricity price of Party A is determined according to the price mechanism agreed in the model text, and the specific price level shall be subject to the retail package confirmed by both parties on the trading platform.

2.  The electricity bill settlement is calculated and settled in RMB, and the grid enterprise shall settle it in accordance with the model text and the rules of the trading platform. Party B shall cooperate to complete the settlement-related work.

3.  Both parties confirm that the measurement, meter reading and settlement cycle of electricity bills are carried out in accordance with the model text and the rules of the trading platform.

III. Rights and Obligations of the Parties

1.  Party A shall ensure that the electricity consumption information provided is true and accurate, and pay the electricity bill as agreed.

2.  Party B shall provide electricity sales services for Party A in accordance with the model text and trading platform rules to protect Party A's electricity rights and interests.

3.  The two parties shall jointly abide by the relevant laws and regulations on electricity transactions and the rules of the trading platform, and cooperate with the power trading center and power grid enterprises to carry out relevant work.

IV. Confidentiality

Both parties shall have the obligation to keep confidential the trade secrets and undisclosed information of the other party learned through the signing and performance of this Confirmation, and shall not disclose them to third parties without the written consent of the other party, except as otherwise provided by laws and regulations or required by the regulatory authorities.

V. Liability for Breach

If any party violates this Confirmation or the agreement of the model text and causes losses to the other party, it shall bear the corresponding liability for compensation in accordance with the law.

VI. Force Majeure

If one party is unable to perform this Confirmation due to force majeure such as earthquakes, typhoons, floods, fires, wars, epidemics, government acts, etc., the affected party shall notify the other party in writing in a timely manner and provide proof within a reasonable period of time, and the two parties shall not be responsible for each other.

VII. Anti-Bribery

In entering into and performing this Confirmation, both parties shall comply strictly with national laws and regulations against commercial bribery. Neither party may offer improper benefits in any form to, or accept them in any form from, the other party's personnel or any relevant third party. If either party breaches this clause, the non-breaching party may terminate this Confirmation and seek compensation for its losses.

VIII. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

1.  The validity, interpretation, change, execution and dispute settlement of this Confirmation shall be governed by the laws of the People's Republic of China.

2.  Any dispute arising out of this Confirmation and the model text or related to this Confirmation shall be resolved by amicable negotiations between the two parties; if the negotiation fails, either party shall have the right to file a lawsuit in the people's court with jurisdiction over the location of Party B.

IX. Miscellaneous

1.  This Confirmation shall take effect from the date of confirmation and seal of both parties on the trading platform. It shall be made in duplicate, and Party A and Party B shall have one copy each, which shall have the same legal effect.

2.  For matters not covered by this Confirmation, the model text and the rules of the trading platform shall prevail.

3.  Without the prior written consent of the other party, neither party shall transfer the rights and obligations under this Confirmation to a third party.

(End of text)
