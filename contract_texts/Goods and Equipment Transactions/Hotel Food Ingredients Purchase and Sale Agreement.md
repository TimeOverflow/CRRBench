Product Purchase and Sale Agreement

Party A: Yunqi Hotel Management Co., Ltd.

Party B (Supplier): Luyuan Foods Co., Ltd.

According to the Civil Code of the People's Republic of China and its relevant laws and regulations, in order to clarify the rights and obligations of Party A and Party B, Party A and Party B reached the following agreement on Party B's supply of products to Party A through amicable consultation:

I. The quality of the product

1. The products supplied by Party B shall meet the national food safety standards and the quality requirements of Party A. Party B shall provide product qualification certificates, inspection reports and relevant qualification documents.

2. The product name, specification, unit price and quantity shall be subject to the Supply List (Annex I) confirmed by both parties.

3. Party B shall ensure that the product is delivered within the warranty period, and the remaining shelf life is not less than two-thirds of the total shelf life.

II. Product delivery

1. Party B shall deliver the products according to the time, place and quantity agreed in the order of Party A, and the delivery location shall be the store warehouse designated by Party A.

2. Party A shall accept the product in time after receiving it, and sign for it if it passes the acceptance test; if the acceptance fails, Party A has the right to refuse to accept it and request Party B to replace or return it.

3. Party B is responsible for the transportation, loading and unloading and insurance of products, and the risk of product damage and deterioration during transportation is borne by Party B.

III. Payment and settlement

1. The total purchase price for the first batch under this Agreement is RMB 86,000.00 (in words: Sixty-Eight Thousand Renminbi Only). Later batches shall be settled at the applicable unit prices based on the actual quantities supplied.

2. Both parties settle accounts according to the natural month. Party B shall provide Party A with the details of the previous month's supply and statement within five working days after the end of each month. After Party A checks, Party B will issue an invoice, and Party A shall pay within 30 working days after receiving the compliant invoice.

3. Party B issues a special VAT invoice, and the applicable tax rate is 9%. Party B collection account information:

IV. Rights and Obligations of Party A

1. Party A shall accept the product in time and pay for the goods as agreed.

2. Party A has the right to randomly inspect the product quality supplied by Party B and require Party B to provide relevant quality inspection reports.

3. Party A has the right to adjust the order quantity according to business needs, but Party B shall be notified in advance.

V. Rights and Obligations of Party B

1. Party B shall ensure that the quality of the products supplied is qualified, the source is legal, and does not infringe on the legitimate rights and interests of any third party.

2. Party B shall deliver the products according to the agreed time, quantity and quality standards, and cooperate with Party A to do a good job of acceptance.

3. Party B shall be fully liable for personal and property damage caused to Party A or a third party due to product quality problems of Party B.

VI. Confidentiality

Both parties shall have the obligation to keep confidential the trade secrets and undisclosed information of the other party learned through the signing and performance of this Agreement, and shall not disclose them to third parties without the written consent of the other party, except as otherwise provided by laws and regulations. After the termination of this Agreement, the confidentiality obligations of both parties shall not be terminated.

VII. Liability for Breach

1. If any party violates this Agreement and causes losses to the other party, it shall bear the corresponding liability for compensation in accordance with the law.

2. If Party B delivers any products late, Party B shall pay Party A liquidated damages equal to 0.05% of the price of the products delivered late for each day of delay. If any products delivered by Party B do not meet the quality requirements, Party A may require replacement or return and claim compensation from Party B for the corresponding losses.

3. If Party A is late in paying for the goods, it shall pay liquidated damages to Party B at 0.05% of the outstanding amount for each day of delay.

VIII. Force Majeure

If one party is unable to perform this Agreement due to force majeure such as earthquakes, typhoons, floods, fires, wars, epidemics, government acts, etc., the party shall notify the other party in writing in a timely manner and provide proof within a reasonable period of time, and the two parties shall not be responsible for each other.

IX. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

1. The validity, interpretation, change, execution and dispute settlement of this Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the People's Republic of China.

2. Any dispute arising out of or related to this Agreement shall be resolved by amicable negotiations between the two parties; if the negotiation fails, either party has the right to file a lawsuit in the people's court with jurisdiction over the location of Party A.

X. Effectiveness and Miscellaneous Provisions

1. This Agreement shall take effect from the date of the seal of both parties and shall be valid for one year. It shall be made in duplicate, and Party A and Party B shall hold one copy each, which shall have the same legal effect.

2. For matters not covered by this Agreement, the two parties may sign a written supplementary agreement separately, and the supplementary agreement shall have the same legal effect as this Agreement.

3. Without the prior written consent of the other party, neither party shall transfer the rights and obligations under this Agreement to a third party.
