In view of:

In response to the country's policy call to strengthen the construction of rural teachers, help rural teachers improve their teaching ability and provide continuous teacher support for schools in remote areas. Qishan Public Welfare Foundation and Nanshan Education Development Foundation help teachers get professional growth and teaching support in rural school scenarios.

After amicable consultation and consensus between the two parties, the following agreement has been signed to jointly carry out the Qishan Public Welfare Rural Teacher Support Plan, and hereby abide by it:

I. Scope of Cooperation

(1) Cooperation project

Party A and Party B jointly launched the "Qishan Public Welfare Rural Teacher Support Program" (hereinafter referred to as "this project") to encourage social forces (rural schools and teacher development centers) to actively and proactively create opportunities and environments for professional growth for rural teachers.

(2) The goal of cooperation

Party A and Party B drive social forces to provide teaching and training, teaching and research guidance and career development guarantees for more rural teachers through positive incentives. During the term of cooperation, the two sides plan to promote the project to 500 rural schools and train at least 6,000 rural teachers.

(3) During the term of cooperation

Project period: from June 1, 2026 to May 31, 2027. After the expiration of the project, if both parties intend to continue to promote the project, they need to sign a separate public welfare project agreement.

(4) Cooperation model

1.  Beneficiaries: rural school teachers who sign up for this project and meet the conditions for participation and the rural schools where the aforementioned teachers are located.

2.  Specific content:

(1)  Party B launched this project initiative for rural schools in cooperative areas, organized teacher training, teaching and research guidance, and evaluated the registered schools according to the project standards. For specific evaluation criteria, please refer to the Project Implementation Manual in Annex III.

(2)  Party A is responsible for providing public welfare financial support for this project, and providing training subsidies and incentives for excellent teachers to registered and eligible rural schools (hereinafter referred to as "beneficiary schools").

(3)  Party A and Party B jointly provide teaching training and insurance coverage for teachers of beneficiary schools.

II. Rights and Obligations of the Parties

(1) Rights and Obligations of Party A

1.  Party A has the right to participate in all actions of this project in the whole or in part, and supervise the implementation of the project, including the implementation of the project and the use of public welfare funds.

2.  Party A has the right to evaluate and accept the implementation effect of this project, and has the right to audit the use of public welfare funds by itself or entrust a third party.

3.  Party A is obliged to provide public welfare funds for this project as agreed.

(2) Rights and Obligations of Party B

1.  Party B shall implement the project in accordance with the agreements and standards of this Agreement and the annex to ensure the smooth achievement of the project goals.

2.  Party B understands and acknowledges that public welfare funds shall only be used for the direct expenses necessary for the implementation of this project, and neither party shall misappropriate, encroach or improperly use public welfare funds through other means.

3.  Party B is obliged to make a comprehensive and complete record of the project implementation and the use of funds, and keep complete accounting records and relevant original receipts and invoices. If Party B fails to use the funds as agreed, Party A has the right to request rectification, and the rectification method shall be implemented in accordance with Article 5, Paragraph 5 of this Agreement.

4.  Party B shall respond positively to the opinions and suggestions put forward by Party A and shall reply in writing within 15 days.

III. Disbursement of Public Welfare Funds

(1) Public welfare funds

The estimated total project budget is RMB 5,600,000.00 (in words: Five Million Six Hundred Thousand Renminbi Only), comprising RMB 3,200,000.00 for teacher training, RMB 1,000,000.00 for guidance on teaching and research, RMB 600,000.00 for teacher insurance premiums, RMB 500,000.00 for outstanding teacher awards, and RMB 300,000.00 for a dedicated audit.

(2) Payment method

1.  Training subsidies are issued by semester. Party B shall provide Party A with a list of beneficiary schools and subsidy details before the 10th working day after the end of each semester. Party A shall confirm within 3 working days from the date of receipt, and pay to the public account designated by Party B within 7 working days after confirmation.

V. Liability for Breach

1.  Party A and Party B should actively promote the project in the mission of assuming social responsibility. If one party breaches the contract during the implementation of the project, it shall bear the corresponding liability for breach of contract and compensate the direct losses caused to the non-breaching party.

2.  If Party B violates national laws or regulations, engages in fraud or diverts project funds to other purposes during the implementation of the Project, Party A may terminate this Agreement immediately and require Party B to return the relevant public welfare funds. Party B shall bear all resulting losses.

3.  If any party unilaterally terminates this Agreement without justifiable reasons, it shall be regarded as a breach of contract, and the breaching party shall pay the corresponding liquidated damages to the non-breaching party and return the corresponding unused public welfare funds.

VI. Limitation of Liability

Party A shall not be liable for any direct or indirect losses to Party B or a third party caused by Party B during the implementation of this project, except for the intentional or gross negligence of Party A.

VII. Anti-Bribery

1.  Party A and Party B agree that they will strictly abide by the laws and regulations of the People's Republic of China on anti-commercial bribery. Both parties know that any form of bribery and corruption will violate the law.

2.  Party A or Party B shall not request, accept, provide or give any benefits from the other party or the other party's operators or other relevant personnel, including but not limited to open deductions, hidden deductions, cash, shopping cards, physical goods, securities, tourism or other intangible benefits.

VIII. Confidentiality

Both parties agree that the undisclosed information and information related to the other party learned by one party through the signing or performance of this Agreement shall be kept confidential and shall not be disclosed to any third party without the consent of the right party (except for disclosures made by relevant laws and regulations or audits required by the competent authorities). After the termination of this Agreement, the confidentiality obligations of both parties shall not be terminated.

IX. Force Majeure

If either party is unable to perform this Agreement due to force majeure, it shall immediately notify the other party in writing and in a timely manner, and shall provide a certificate issued by the relevant government department or notary public within 15 days after the occurrence of force majeure. If the problem cannot be solved within 30 days after the force majeure occurs, either party may choose to terminate the agreement with a written notice in advance.

X. Termination of the Agreement

1.  This Agreement can be terminated in advance in the following ways: (1) Terminate this Agreement in advance by mutual agreement in writing; (2) If one party enters into bankruptcy application or liquidation proceedings, the other party has the right to terminate this Agreement.

2.  If this Agreement is terminated for any reason, it shall not affect the rights and obligations arising from both parties under this Agreement before the termination date of this Agreement.

XI. Miscellaneous Provisions

1.  This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the People's Republic of China. If there is any dispute or dispute, the two parties shall resolve it through amicable consultation; if there is no consensus, either party shall have the right to file a lawsuit in the people's court with jurisdiction over the defendant's location.

2.  This Agreement takes effect on the date both parties affix their seals. It is executed in four counterparts of equal legal effect, two for each party.
