4. Payment Terms

Company shall pay the Sponsorship Fee of US $30,000 within thirty (30) days of the Effective Date by wire transfer to the bank account designated in writing by ICSA. 10. Assignment

Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party, except that Company may assign this Agreement to an affiliate without such consent.

11. Dispute Resolution

Any dispute, controversy or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, or the making, interpretation, construction, performance or breach of this Agreement, shall be finally settled by binding arbitration conducted in English within the State of California. The arbitration shall be administered by JAMS pursuant to its Streamlined Arbitration Rules and Procedures, before three (3) neutral arbitrators, with each Party appointing one arbitrator of its choosing and those two arbitrators selecting the third.
