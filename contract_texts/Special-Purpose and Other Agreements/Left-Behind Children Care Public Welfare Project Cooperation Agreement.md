In view of:

In response to the country's policy call to strengthen the care and protection of left-behind children in rural areas, to help left-behind children broaden the channels for growth and companionship, and to provide convenience for rural children who lack family care. Qishan Public Welfare Foundation and Yunfan Community Services Co., Ltd. help left-behind children get better growth care and learning support in rural community scenes.

After amicable consultation and consensus between the two parties, the following agreement has been signed to jointly carry out the public welfare project of Qishan Public Welfare Yunfan Left-behind Children Care Project, so as to jointly abide by it:

I. Scope of Cooperation

(1) Cooperation project

Party A and Party B jointly launched the "Qishan Public Welfare Yunfan Left-behind Children Care Project" (hereinafter referred to as "this project") to encourage social forces (rural community service stations) to actively and actively create opportunities and environments for left-behind children to accompany their growth.

(2) The goal of cooperation

Through positive incentives, Party A and Party B drive social forces (Yunfan Village Service Station) to provide after-school companionship, psychological counseling and learning guidance for more left-behind children. During the term of cooperation, the two sides plan to promote the project to 800 rural communities and promote the cumulative service stations to serve at least 12,000 left-behind children.

(3) During the term of cooperation

Project period: from May 1, 2026 to April 30, 2027. After the expiration of the project, if both parties intend to continue to promote the project, they need to sign a separate public welfare project agreement.

(4) Cooperation model

1.  Beneficiaries: rural community service stations that sign up for this project and meet the conditions for participation and the left-behind children actually served by the aforementioned service stations.

2.  Specific content:

(1)  Party B launched this project initiative for rural communities in the cooperative area to guide the construction of service stations, train volunteer counselors, and evaluate the registration service stations according to the project standards.

(2)  Party A is responsible for providing public welfare financial support for this project, and providing operational subsidies and excellent station review incentives for registered and eligible rural community service stations (hereinafter referred to as "beneficiary sites").

(3)  Party A and Party B jointly provide training and insurance coverage for the volunteer counselors of the beneficiary site (hereinafter referred to as the "Counselor Guarantee Plan").

II. Rights and Obligations of the Parties

(1) Rights and Obligations of Party A

1.  Party A has the right to participate in all actions of the project in the whole process or in part, supervise the implementation of the project, including the implementation of the project and the use of public welfare funds, and put forward opinions and suggestions for improvement in the project process.

2.  Party A has the right to evaluate and accept the implementation effect of this project, and has the right to audit the use of public welfare funds and the authenticity and legality of relevant bills by itself or entrust a third party.

3.  Party A is obliged to provide public welfare funds for this project as agreed.

(2) Rights and Obligations of Party B

1.  Party B shall implement the project in accordance with the agreements and standards of this Agreement and the annex to ensure the smooth achievement of the project goals. If there is any adjustment or change in the project, it shall be explained in writing to Party A 15 days in advance, and it can only be implemented after obtaining the written consent of Party A.

2.  Party B understands and understands that public welfare funds shall only be used for the direct expenses necessary for the implementation of this project, and neither party shall misappropriate, encroach or improperly use public welfare funds through other means for any reason.

3.  Party B is obliged to record the implementation of the project and the use of funds in a comprehensive and complete way, and keep complete accounting records and relevant original vouchers and invoices for Party A or third-party review.

4.  Party B shall respond positively to the opinions and suggestions put forward by Party A and shall reply in writing within 15 days.

IX. Force Majeure

If either party is unable to perform this Agreement due to force majeure, it shall immediately notify the other party in writing and in a timely manner, and shall provide a certificate issued by the relevant government department or notary public within 15 days after the occurrence of force majeure. If the problem cannot be solved within 30 days after the force majeure occurs, either party may choose to terminate the agreement with a written notice in advance.

X. Termination of the Agreement

1.  This Agreement can be terminated in advance in the following ways: (1) Terminate this Agreement in advance by mutual agreement in writing; (2) If one party enters into bankruptcy application or liquidation proceedings, the other party has the right to terminate this Agreement.

2.  If this Agreement is terminated for any reason, it shall not affect the rights and obligations arising from both parties under this Agreement before the termination date of this Agreement.

1.  This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the People's Republic of China. If there is any dispute or dispute during the implementation of this project or the performance of this Agreement, the two parties shall resolve it through amicable consultation; if there is no consensus, either party shall have the right to file a lawsuit in the people's court with jurisdiction over the defendant's location.

2.  This Agreement takes effect on the date both parties affix their seals. It is executed in four counterparts of equal legal effect, two for each party.
