Party B: Zhuhai Xiangzhou Lanhai Electrical Appliance Sales Co., Ltd.

After equal and voluntary consultation, Parties A and B have reached an agreement on Party A's provision of venue use services for Party B, and hereby conclude this Agreement.

# I. Basic Information

1. Party A agrees to let Party B use the designated atrium space in Building 1 of Hongsheng Commercial Plaza (the "Property"), at No. 1288 Qinglü Middle Road, Xiangzhou District, Zhuhai, to display Party B's goods or services. The specific space in the common area (the "Venue") is shown in the attached drawing.
2. Party B knows that the site is a public area and promises not to carry out any decoration or install any fixtures on it, and ensures that the materials used in the site (including but not limited to objects, items, materials, pictures or facilities, etc., hereinafter referred to as "such temporary facilities") meet the requirements of government departments such as fire fighting.
3. Before the signing of this Agreement, Party B has checked and fully understood the relevant local policies and Party A's business scope, qualifications, etc., and also expected the commercial, legal and policy risks in the performance of this Agreement. There is no misunderstanding or omission of the content involved in this Agreement.

# II. The term of the agreement

1. From 00:00 on June 18, 2026 to 24:00 on June 22, 2026;

2. If the site cannot continue to be used by Party B due to changes in national laws and regulations, relevant government departments or the requirements of Party A, Party A has the right to notify Party B to terminate this Agreement in advance at any time. This Agreement will be terminated in advance on the date of confirmation of Party A's written notice. All expenses under this Agreement shall be settled based on actual amounts, and Party A shall not bear any responsibility to Party B;

3. After the expiration of the period of use/the early termination or cancellation of this Agreement, Party B has no right to continue to use the site.

When the deposit is not enough to pay for the recourse of such third parties, Party B should actively settle the problem and pay the corresponding fees. If Party A suffers any loss as a result, Party A has the right to recover the loss from Party B as a debt.

4. Within 6 months from the date of expiration or early termination of this Agreement, if Party B does not receive any third party's complaints, claims or claims about Party B's activities at the site, and Party B has fully and properly fulfilled all the agreements of this Agreement, Party B shall submit a written application for the refund of the deposit in the 7th month, and Party A shall return the deposit or the rest to Party B without interest within 30 working days from the date of receipt of the written application. Party B also needs to hold the original receipt issued by Party A when submitting a written application, otherwise Party A has the right to refuse to accept the refund and shall not bear any liability for breach of contract.

# V. Rights and Obligations of Party A

1. Party A has the right to request Party B to evacuate, move and cover such temporary facilities at any time. Party B agrees to cooperate and has no objection. Party A does not need to bear any responsibility for this. If Party B cannot use the site normally due to Party A's reasons within the term of this Agreement, Party A shall refund the paid fee to Party B.
2. Party A has the right to comment on Party B's behavior at the venue, and Party B shall cooperate.

# VI. Rights and Obligations of Party B

1. Party B undertakes to ensure that Party B's employees, employees and partners comply with the property management system formulated and modified by the property management company hired by Party A for the property (hereinafter referred to as the "property company").

2. If the site or its ancillary facilities/equipment is damaged due to Party B, Party B shall be responsible for the repair within the period specified by Party A, or pay the repair cost, and compensate Party A and/or the losses caused by the third party. Otherwise, Party A has the right to terminate this Agreement in advance, take back the site and confiscate the unused fees paid by Party B without any liability to Party B.

3. Party B agrees that Party A/Property Company has the right to maintain, repair, decorate and perform other reasonable acts on the site or ancillary facilities/equipment at any time.

4. Party B shall not give it any rights under this Agreement, regardless of whether such acts are paid or not.

5. Party B shall undertake to have the legal ownership and use rights of the materials, trademarks (including but not limited to graphics, text), enterprise names or trade names and event pictures, etc. (hereinafter referred to as "information materials") used on the site, and shall not infringe on the legitimate rights and interests of any third party (including but not limited to intellectual property rights, property rights, personal rights, portrait rights, etc.). At the same time, Party A has the right to use such information materials for the publicity materials of the property free of charge. If any third party claims any rights or claims compensation from Party A about the materials, trademarks, activities, etc. used by Party B on the site, Party B shall settle it. If Party A suffers any losses due to cooperation with Party B, Party B will bear full liability.

6. Party B shall take reasonable measures to reduce and avoid the impact on surrounding merchants and residents, including but not limited to the sound volume, etc., and obey the management of Party A/Property Company. If the surrounding merchants and residents raise complaints, Party B shall properly resolve them and save Party A from any losses.
7. Party B shall not do any act that may affect the fire protection, host, mechanical and electrical system. Otherwise, Party A has the right to terminate this Contract in advance, take back the site and require Party B to bear the restoration costs and losses.
8. Party B's activities at the venue shall comply with laws and regulations. If Party B needs to apply for relevant licenses, examination and approval, approval or filing with government departments such as industry and commerce, taxation, planning, construction, environmental protection, fire protection and public security for its activities, Party B shall handle it by itself and bear the corresponding costs. If there is any violation, Party B agrees to bear the corresponding responsibility.
9．Party B undertakes not to engage in on-site sales or acts/activity that can be considered sales at the venue.
10. If any third party's personal or property damage is caused by Party B, Party B shall bear the corresponding legal responsibility and liability for compensation.

# VII. Party B Item Requirements

1. Party B can only place such temporary facilities approved by Party A on the site. Such temporary facilities themselves and their placement manner and location shall strictly comply with the plan approved by Party A.
2. Party A's approval is only considered in terms of aesthetics, and there is no need to be responsible for the legality of such temporary facilities. The examination and approval of Party A is also not a defense to mitigate and exempt Party B from liability. Party B needs to be independently responsible for such temporary facilities. If Party B does not submit the plan in advance or the plan submitted in advance does not pass the review of Party A, Party A has the right to terminate this Agreement in advance without any liability to Party B.

3. Party B shall ensure that all such temporary facilities:

1) It has been prefabricated before being transported to the site, and all the preparations for placement (including but not limited to processing, assembly and painting) have been completed;
2) It is a light, movable and easy-to-pack, handle and transport items;
3) In any aspect (including but not limited to labor employment, production, transportation, storage, quality, composition, specifications, standards, labeling, packaging, testing, installation) comply with national laws and regulations and industry rules and standards, especially fire protection, environmental protection, occupational health, safe production and other provisions, and obtain relevant safety qualification certificates;
4) Its own, raw materials, auxiliary materials, production process, appearance and design do not infringe the trademarks, copyrights, patent rights or other intellectual property rights of any third party;
5) It will not cover other stores after being placed.

# VIII. Exemption and limitation of liability

1. Party B agrees that Party A shall not be liable for any losses incurred under the following circumstances, except for the effective judicial ruling (and any fees paid by Party B under this Agreement shall not be reduced or suspended):

1) Any losses caused by Party B and/or Party B's employees, members, customers, etc. due to the repair, maintenance or temporary suspension of public facilities not directly due to the fault of Party A, or the temporary interruption of water, electricity, network, telephone, fax or other related services or supply of the site;
2) Any injury or loss to Party B or a third party due to Party B or a third party;
3) Any losses caused by force majeure such as typhoons, earthquakes, floods, lightning or terrorist attacks and wars;

2. Any losses caused by Party B's violation of this Agreement.

3. Party A/The bodyguards, managers, mechanical and electronic anti-theft systems of any nature (if any) provided by the property company do not constitute the obligation of Party A/Property Company to be responsible for the security and custody of Party B's property. Party B shall be responsible for the property or personal safety of Party B or Party B team members in the venue at any time.
4. Party A may provide relevant services to Party B and Party B's competitors at the same time. Party B agrees to the possible behavior of Party A and expressly exempts any actual or potential conflicts of interest.

# IX. Termination of service relationship

1. After the expiration, early termination and early cancellation of this Agreement, Party B shall return the site and Party A shall have the right to take back the site. Party B shall ensure that all Party B's equipment, facilities and items in the site shall be removed, and the site shall be clean as a whole. Otherwise, Party B shall compensate all the repair/cleaning costs according to the quoted amount of Party A.
2. If Party B does not fulfill the obligations of paragraph 1 of this Article, after the expiration of the term of this Agreement or the date of early termination, if there are any items left by Party B (hereinafter referred to as "remaining items") in the site, Party B shall be deemed to have waived the ownership of the remaining items and transferred them to Party A free of charge. Party A has the right to dispose of the leftovers in the manner it deems appropriate and to charge Party B the fees incurred in the disposal of the leftovers.

# X. Party B breach of contract

1. If Party B pays late, sets off or refuses to pay any amount due under this Agreement for any reason, Party A may demand prompt payment. For each day of delay, Party A may charge Party B liquidated damages of three per mille (3‰) of the overdue amount. If payment remains overdue for more than fourteen (14) days, Party A may exercise any one or more of the following rights concurrently or separately:

1) Immediately stop Party B's use of the site without prior notice (including but not limited to cutting off the supply of water, electricity, communication, etc. of the site) until Party B's breach of contract is corrected;
2) Terminate this Agreement in advance and take back the site.
2. Party B shall return the site on time and in accordance with the conditions agreed in this Agreement. If Party B fails to return the site on time or in accordance with the conditions agreed in this Agreement, Party A shall have the right to charge Party B liquidated damages of RMB 1,500 for each hour of delay and require Party B to compensate Party A for all losses suffered as a result.
3. If an employee or employee of Party B commits bad acts such as beating, insulting consumers or Party A/employee of a property company, the employee shall compensate the victim of the aforementioned incident and the direct economic losses suffered by Party A, and Party B shall be jointly and severally liable for the aforementioned losses.
4. Unauthorized sales. If Party B engages in on-site sales or acts/activity that can be considered to be considered sales at the venue without authorization, Party A has the right to request Party B to stop immediately. If Party B fails to perform, Party A has the right to terminate this Agreement in advance.
5. If the breaching party causes losses to the non-breaching party, in addition to bearing the corresponding liability for breach of contract, it shall also be liable for compensation.

# XI. Insurance

Party B shall be liable for the losses suffered by any person in the venue, except for Party A's direct fault. Party B shall purchase public liability insurance (indicating that Party A is a joint insured and adding cross-liability clauses and waiver of the right of subrogation). Party B shall provide a copy of the valid insurance policy to Party A before using the venue.
