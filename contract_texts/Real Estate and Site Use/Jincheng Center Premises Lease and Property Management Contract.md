# Part II　General Lease Terms

# I. Rental housing situation

1. For the specific information of Party B leasing Party A project housing, please refer to the main lease conditions in the first part. Party B has inspected the house on the spot and expressed satisfaction and acceptance of its location and conditions.
2. At the time of signing this Contract, please refer to Article 2 of the main lease conditions of Part I for details of the real estate certification of the project.

# II. Purpose and house name

1. The house is used as an office space, and Party B is not allowed to change it without authorization.
2. Party B shall take the name listed in Article 3 of the main lease conditions of Part I as the marking name of the house.

# III. Lease

1. The lease period is listed in Article 4 of the main lease conditions of Part I. The expiration of the lease period does not exempt the obligations that the two parties have not fulfilled before the expiration of the lease period.
2. At the expiration of the lease period, if Party B needs to renew the lease, it shall submit a written application to Party A 90 days before the expiration of the lease period, and the two parties shall sign a separate lease renewal contract by consensus.

# IV. The delivery of the house

1. Party A shall deliver the house together with the existing decoration and facilities (see Annex II for details) to Party B on the delivery date.
2. At the time of delivery, both parties shall jointly accept and sign the handover form as Annex III of this Contract.

# V. Party A's responsibility for maintenance

Party A is responsible for the maintenance of the main structure, public areas and public facilities of the house to ensure that the house is in a normal usable state. Party A shall notify Party B in advance for maintenance, except in cases of emergency.

# VI. Party B's repair and maintenance responsibility

Party B is responsible for the daily maintenance of non-public facilities inside the house. If the damage is caused by Party B's improper use, Party B shall be responsible for the repair or bear the repair cost.

# VII. Responsibility for safety management

Party B shall abide by the safety management system formulated by Party A and the property management of the project, and implement safety responsibilities such as fire protection and public security. Party B shall sign Annex IV "Commitment on Responsibility for Safety Management".

# VIII. The return of the house

If the lease period expires or this Contract is terminated or cancelled in advance, Party B shall restore the house and its ancillary facilities to the state at the time of delivery (except for normal loss) on the return date and return it to Party A, and settle all payable expenses at the same time.

# IX. Rent, property management fee and payment

1. For details of rent, property management fee standards and payment methods, please refer to Article 5 of the main lease conditions in the first part.
2. Party B shall pay various fees according to the cycle, manner and time node agreed in this Contract.

# X. Deposit

For details of the payment, deduction and refund of the deposit, please refer to Article 6 of the main lease conditions of Part I. After Party A deducts the deposit in accordance with the contract, Party B shall replenish the deposit within seven days after receiving the written notice.

# XI. Transfer and sublease

1. Without the prior written consent of Party A, Party B shall not sublet or sublet all or part of the house, and shall not transfer the rights and obligations under this Contract to a third party.
2. If Party A transfers the ownership of the project, it shall guarantee that the transferee will continue to perform this Contract, which shall be binding on the transferee. Party B enjoys the right of priority to lease under the same conditions.

# XII. The rights and obligations of Party A

1. Party A has the right to collect rent and related fees as agreed.
2. Party A shall guarantee the normal use of the house by Party B during the lease period, and shall not interfere with the use of Party B for non-legal or agreed reasons.
3. Party A shall fulfill the maintenance obligations stipulated in this Contract.

# XIII. The rights and obligations of Party B

1. Party B has the right to use the house normally for the agreed purpose during the lease period.
2. Party B shall pay the rent, property management fee, utility fee and deposit on time.
3. Party B shall use the house legally, shall not engage in illegal activities, and shall not store dangerous goods.

# XIV. Exonerate

If the house cannot be used normally or this Contract cannot be performed due to force majeure, government acts or municipal construction and other reasons other than Party A's fault, the two parties shall not be liable for breach of contract, and the relevant expenses shall be settled based on actual amounts.

# XV. Insurance

Party B shall take out the corresponding insurance for its property and business activities in the house. Party A has taken out the corresponding insurance for the public area of the project.

# XVI. Party B breach of contract

1. If Party B is overdue for paying rent, property management fees and other payables, it shall pay liquidated damages to Party A at 0.05% of the overdue amount for each day of delay.
2. If Party B has not paid for more than 30 days, Party A has the right to notify Party B in writing to terminate this Contract, take back the house, and deduct arrears and liquidated damages from the deposit.
3. If Party B changes the purpose of the house without authorization, subleases without authorization or engages in illegal activities, Party A has the right to terminate this Contract and require Party B to compensate for the loss.

# XVII. Party A breach of contract

1. If Party A fails to deliver the house as agreed or fails to fulfill its maintenance obligations, causing Party B to be unable to use it normally, the corresponding rent shall be reduced according to the actual impact period, and Party B shall be compensated for the direct losses suffered as a result.
2. If Party A takes back the house in advance for reasons other than the law or agreed, the rent and deposit paid for the remaining period shall be refunded, and the liquidated damages shall be paid to Party B at the standard of one month's rent.

# XVIII. Terminate the contract

1. This Contract may be terminated by mutual agreement between the two parties.
2. In the event of the termination stipulated in this Contract, the non-breaching party shall have the right to notify the breaching party in writing to terminate this Contract.
3. If this Contract is canceled or terminated, the two parties shall settle the expenses truthfully, and Party B shall return the house as agreed.

# XIX. Notify

Notices and documents related to this Contract shall be in writing and sent to the contact address listed in Schedule I by special person, express delivery or e-mail. If it is delivered by a special person, it shall be deemed to have been delivered when it is signed for; if it is sent by express delivery, it shall be deemed to have been delivered on the third day after delivery; if it is sent by e-mail, it shall be deemed to have been delivered when it is sent successfully. If the contact information of one party changes, it shall notify the other party in writing in advance.

# XX. Taxes

The taxes and fees payable by the two parties for the signing and performance of this Contract shall be borne by each other in accordance with laws and regulations.

# 21. Applicable law and dispute settlement

1. The validity, interpretation, change, execution and dispute settlement of this Contract shall be governed by the laws of the People's Republic of China.
2. Any dispute arising out of or related to this Contract shall be resolved by amicable negotiations between the two parties; if the negotiation fails, either party shall have the right to file a lawsuit in the people's court with jurisdiction over the location of Party A.

# 22. Confidentiality

Both parties have the obligation to keep confidential the trade secrets and undisclosed information of the other party learned through the signing and performance of this Contract, and shall not disclose them to third parties without the written consent of the other party, except as otherwise provided by laws and regulations or required by the regulatory authorities. The obligation of this article shall not be invalidated by the cancellation or termination of this Contract.

# 23. Force Majeure

1. If one party is unable to perform or fully perform this Contract due to force majeure such as earthquakes, typhoons, floods, fires, wars, epidemics, government acts, etc., the party experiencing force majeure shall notify the other party in writing in a timely manner and provide a certificate issued by the competent institution within a reasonable period of time.
2. Both parties shall not be responsible for losses caused by force majeure. If the impact of force majeure lasts for more than 90 days, either party has the right to notify the other party in writing to terminate this Contract.

# 24. Other

1. For matters not covered by this Contract, the two parties may sign a written supplementary agreement separately, which shall have the same legal effect as this Contract.
2. The terms of this Contract are independent of each other, and the invalidity of some clauses does not affect the validity of the remaining provisions.
3. This Contract takes effect on the date both parties sign it and affix their official company seals or designated contract seals. It is executed in four counterparts of equal legal effect, two for each party.

# 25. Anti-Bribery

1. In entering into and performing this Contract, both parties shall comply strictly with national laws and regulations against commercial bribery. Neither party may offer kickbacks, cash gifts, securities, valuables or other improper benefits in any form to, or accept them in any form from, the other party's personnel or any relevant third party.
2. If any party violates the above agreement, the non-breaching party shall have the right to terminate this Contract and require the breaching party to compensate for all losses caused thereby.
