# I.  Definitions

1) Laundry equipment business: Party B provides apartment residents with self-service washing, drying and online payment through the Internet of Things technology. 2) Point: Party A provides Party B with a place to place laundry equipment. Place 1 main console and 4 washing and drying machines, single specification: 0.85m\*0.6m\*1.2m.

# II.  Term of Cooperation

1) The term of cooperation is from April 1, 2026 to March 31, 2029; 30 days before the expiration of the term of cooperation, the two parties shall jointly negotiate the renewal of the contract.

The site shall be well ventilated, have adequate drainage, be readily accessible to residents and meet appropriate hygiene conditions. Flammable materials shall not be allowed to accumulate at or near the installation site.

# IV.  Rights and Obligations of Party A

1) Party A shall guarantee that it has the right or has been legally authorized to sign and perform this Agreement. If Party A's aforementioned qualifications change, Party B shall be notified in writing within 30 days from the date of knowledge, and cooperate with Party B to deal with the corresponding follow-up matters.

2) Party A shall provide stable power supply and water supply to ensure that the agreed point of the laundry equipment is not relocated; within one month after Party A's written notice, Party B shall adjust the laundry equipment to the location designated by Party A in the building. If Party B does not agree to the adjustment, the two parties shall terminate this Agreement in advance, which shall not constitute a breach of contract. After the termination, the two parties shall settle the service fee according to the actual number of days of use, which shall be implemented in accordance with paragraph 9 of Article 7.

3) If abnormal situations such as failure, damage or user complaints of the laundry equipment are found, the on-site maintainer of Party B shall be notified in time, and Party B will send someone to inspect and repair.

4) During the term of this Agreement, Party A guarantees that Party B enjoys the exclusive right to use the site and shall not use the agreed site for any other purpose or provide it to any third party outside this Agreement.

5) Without the written consent of the other party, neither party shall disclose the content of this Agreement and confidential information related to business models, business plans, etc. disclosed by the other party during the cooperation process to any third party.

# V.  Party B Rights and Obligations

1) Where both parties are performing this Agreement as agreed, Party B shall pay the fees to Party A when due. If Party B fails to pay on time, Party B shall pay Party A liquidated damages of 0.4% of the outstanding overdue amount per day.

2) Party B enjoys the exclusive right to use the site and has the right to use laundry equipment to carry out business including self-service washing and drying, fuselage advertising (if any), etc. within the scope permitted by law. Party B promises that the business it carries out will not affect the normal order in the property area of Party A, and the advertising content will be legal and civilized.

3) Party B and its designated operating company shall abide by the provisions of the state's laws and regulations on fire fighting, flood control, environmental protection, safety, etc., and shall also abide by Party A's regulations on site safety management and property management specifications related to the site safety in this Agreement.

4) The laundry equipment installed and placed by Party B should be fixed, stable and strong, and its firmness should be checked regularly; Party B and its designated partners shall be responsible for the daily operation of the laundry equipment, including the maintenance of equipment hygiene, daily maintenance, etc. Party A shall assist in cleaning up the dirt on the ground near the equipment location.

5) Party B is responsible for the daily customer service of laundry equipment.

6) Party B shall not use the premises of Party A to engage in any illegal activities. If Party B, Party B or other third parties are injured, economic losses or punished by government departments due to the illegal use of the laundry equipment, safety, hygiene and other problems, the designated company shall be fully liable for compensation to the injured party, and Party B shall be jointly and severally liable.

# VI.  Effectiveness, cancellation and termination of the agreement

1）This Agreement shall take effect from the date of seal of both parties.

2）If the two parties agree and terminate this Agreement, they shall not be liable for breach of contract. After the cancellation or termination of this Agreement, Party B shall move away from the laundry equipment within 10 working days, and the two parties shall settle the expenses under this Agreement truthfully.

3） During the implementation of this Agreement, if the following circumstances occur, one party has the right to terminate this Agreement within 30 days from the date of written notice: from the date of written notice by Party A, if Party B has not paid for more than 30 days, or Party A proposes to terminate this Agreement, Party A has the right to terminate the contract; Party B has the right to terminate the contract due to the reduction of the size of residents in the site or the inability to supply water and electricity; after the termination, the two parties shall settle the service fee according to the actual number of days of use.

# VII.  Miscellaneous

1) For matters not covered by this Agreement, the two parties shall negotiate and sign a written supplementary agreement, and the supplementary agreement shall have the same legal effect as this Agreement.

2) Party B has the right to unilaterally transfer the rights and obligations under this Agreement to the related party. Party B shall notify Party A in writing and sign a supplementary agreement for the specific transfer arrangements such as the transferee and the transfer date. In addition, Party B shall not transfer all or part of the rights and obligations under this Contract to a third party without the prior written consent of Party A.

3) Notices, documents, etc. related to this Agreement shall be sent in writing by e-mail or by recognized express service to the address listed on the home page of this Agreement. If it is sent by e-mail, the time when the email arrives at the recipient's designated email address is the notification delivery time; if it is mailed by express mail, the fourth day from the date of sending shall be regarded as the date of delivery.

4) This Agreement is made in three copies, Party A holds two copies and Party B holds one copy, and each copy has the same legal effect. The annex is an integral part of this Agreement and has the same legal effect as this Agreement.

5) Without the prior written consent of the other party, neither party shall directly or indirectly, partially or in whole use the trademarks, patents, logos and other intellectual property rights of the other party and its affiliates. Otherwise, if it causes losses to the other party, it shall be liable for compensation.

6) If this Agreement is terminated in advance or due to expiration for any reason, Party B shall be responsible for dismantling the laundry equipment within [10] working days from the date of cancellation/termination of this Contract, and be responsible for restoring the site occupied by the laundry equipment to its original state. Otherwise, Party A has the right to dismantle the laundry equipment, and the resulting demolition costs shall be borne by Party B.

7) If one party is unable to perform this Agreement due to force majeure such as earthquakes, typhoons, floods, fires, epidemics or government acts, the affected party shall notify the other party in writing in a timely manner and provide proof within a reasonable period, and the two parties shall not be responsible for each other.

8) Party B shall ensure that its designated company is aware of and complies with the agreement of this Agreement. If the designated company violates this Agreement and causes breach of contract or other liability, Party B shall be jointly and severally liable.
