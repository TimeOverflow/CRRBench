# CONFIDENTIALITY AGREEMENT 保密协议 KBL-BD-26-018

Beidou Yunxin Technology Co., Ltd. ("you") hereby agree as follows: In contemplation of entering into a potential business relationship (the "Relationship") with Kato Branding (Shanghai) Co., Ltd. ("KBL"), and during the course of any such Relationship, you may have access to KBL's confidential information ("Confidential Information," as further defined below). You agree that your receipt of the Confidential Information is subject to the following terms:

北斗云信科技有限公司（"您"）谨此同意如下：在考虑与卡拓品牌策划（上海）有限公司（"KBL"）建立潜在业务关系（"关系"）以及在此类关系存续期间，您可能获取 KBL 的保密信息（"保密信息"，详细定义参见下文）。您同意，接收保密信息受下列条款约束：

1. DEFINITION OF "CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION". "Confidential Information" means information and materials that you know or have reason to know, by the nature or circumstances surrounding their disclosure, should be treated as confidential, whether in written, visual, oral, electronic, web-based, or other form, and whether disclosed by KBL or its representatives. Confidential Information includes, without limitation, marketing, design, product, customer, technical, personnel, and business information.

保密信息的定义. "保密信息"系指您依据披露性质或情形而知悉或有理由知悉的应当保秘对待的信息，无论是否采取书面、图像、口头、电子、网络或其他形式，无论是否由 KBL 或其代表披露。保密信息包括但不限于营销、设计、产品、客户、技术、人员和业务信息。

2. EXCEPTIONS TO CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION. Confidential Information does not include information that: (a) is in or enters the public domain through no act or omission of yours; (b) you lawfully receive from a third party without restriction on use or disclosure; (c) you knew prior to receiving Confidential Information from KBL; or (d) you independently develop without use of, or access to, any Confidential Information.

保密信息例外情况. 保密信息不包括下列信息：(a) 非因您的不作为或过失而进入公众领域；(b) 您向第三方合法获取，且无使用或披露限制；(c) 您在向 KBL 获取保密信息前已知悉；或 (d) 由您在未使用或获取任何保密信息的情况下独立创造。

3. YOUR OBLIGATIONS. You shall hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence and shall not disclose, reproduce, distribute, or otherwise exploit any Confidential Information except solely for the purpose of evaluating the Relationship. You shall limit access to the Confidential Information to those of your employees who have a need to know and who are bound by written confidentiality obligations at least as protective as those set out herein. You shall use no less than reasonable care to protect the Confidential Information.

您的义务. 您应对全部保密信息予以严格保密，除仅为评估本关系之目的外，不得披露、复制、分发或以其他任何方式利用任何保密信息。您应将保密信息的访问限于确有必要知悉且受书面保密义务约束的员工。您应以不低于合理谨慎的标准保护保密信息。

4. TERM. Your confidentiality obligations under this agreement shall commence on the date you first receive any Confidential Information and shall continue for a period of five (5) years from the date of disclosure of the relevant Confidential Information, or indefinitely with respect to any Confidential Information that constitutes a trade secret under applicable law.

期限. 您在本协议项下的保密义务自您首次收到任何保密信息之日起开始，并自相关保密信息披露之日起持续五（5）年；对于构成适用法律项下商业秘密的保密信息，保密义务无限期存续。

5. LIQUIDATED DAMAGES. You acknowledge that any unauthorized use or disclosure of the Confidential Information will cause KBL irreparable harm for which monetary damages alone would be inadequate. Accordingly, you shall compensate KBL for liquidated damages based on KBL's estimation at KBL's absolute discretion, and the amount of liquidated damages for each breach shall be at least RMB three hundred thousand yuan (RMB 300,000). KBL shall further be entitled to seek injunctive relief from any court of competent jurisdiction without the necessity of posting a bond.

违约金. 您确认，任何未经授权的使用或披露保密信息的行为将给 KBL 造成无法弥补的损害，仅靠金钱赔偿不足以救济。因此，您应按 KBL 自行酌情估算向 KBL 赔偿违约金，且每次违约的违约金金额应不低于人民币叁拾万元整（RMB 300,000）。KBL 还有权向任何有管辖权的法院申请禁止令救济，而无须提供担保。

6. RETURN OF INFORMATION. Upon the cessation of discussions with respect to the Relationship, or at any time upon KBL's written request, you shall immediately return all Confidential Information (including all copies, summaries, and extracts) to KBL, or at KBL's option, destroy such Confidential Information and certify such destruction in writing.

信息的返还. 在关于本关系的磋商终止时，或在 KBL 书面要求的任何时候，您应立即向 KBL 返还全部保密信息（包括所有副本、摘要及节选），或按 KBL 的选择销毁该等保密信息并书面证明已销毁。

7. NO LICENSE; NO OBLIGATION. Nothing in this agreement grants you any right, title, or interest in or to any Confidential Information, or any license under any patent, trademark, copyright, or other intellectual property of KBL. KBL may terminate discussions with respect to the Relationship at any time without prior notice and without any liability.

无许可；无义务. 本协议的任何内容均不授予您对任何保密信息的任何权利、所有权或权益，亦不授予您在 KBL 任何专利、商标、著作权或其他知识产权项下的许可。KBL 可随时终止关于本关系的磋商，无须事先通知，亦无须承担任何责任。

8. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION. This agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the People's Republic of China. Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this agreement shall be submitted to the people's court having jurisdiction at the place where the defendant is domiciled.

法律适用及争议解决. 本协议受中华人民共和国法律管辖并据其解释。因本协议引起的或与本协议有关的任何争议，均应提交被告住所地有管辖权的人民法院诉讼解决。

9. ENTIRE AGREEMENT; ASSIGNMENT. This agreement constitutes the entire agreement between you and KBL with respect to the subject matter hereof. Neither Party may assign this agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party, except that either Party may assign this agreement to an affiliate without such consent.

完整协议；转让. 本协议构成您与 KBL 之间就本协议标的事项达成的完整协议。未经另一方事先书面同意，任何一方不得转让本协议，但任何一方均可无须该同意即将本协议转让给其关联方。

You have read and understood all the terms of this agreement and agree to be bound by them.

您已阅读并理解本协议的全部条款，并同意受其约束。
