After amicable negotiations, Party A and Party B reached an agreement on matters related to the cooperation agreement of the Information System Integration Project (Bid Section I) of Wuzhong Smart Park, and signed this Contract in accordance with the Civil Code of the People's Republic of China and other relevant laws and regulations.

Article 1 Project Overview

1.1 Party B provides Party A with equipment supply, system integration, software deployment, commissioning and after-sales maintenance services for the Wuzhong Smart Park Information System Integration Project (Bid Section I).

1.2 For the specific equipment list, software functions, technical parameters and project volume, please refer to Annex II "List of Equipment and Project Volume".

1.3 If Party A increases or decreases other equipment or services, the two parties shall sign a supplementary agreement separately.

Article 2 Contract Price and Payment

2.1 The total Contract price is RMB 1,860,000.00 (in words: One Million Six Hundred Thousand Renminbi Only), inclusive of tax and all costs, including equipment, software, integration, commissioning, training, warranty, and taxes and charges.

2.2 Payment progress: 30% will be paid after the contract is signed and effective, 40% will be paid after the equipment is accepted, 25% will be paid after the final inspection of the system, and 5% will be paid after the warranty period expires and there is no quality problem.

2.3 Party B shall issue a special VAT invoice of the corresponding amount to Party A before Party A pays each amount. The applicable tax rate is 13%, otherwise Party A has the right to delay payment and shall not be liable for breach of contract. Party A shall pay within 7 working days after the payment terms are met and the invoice for the corresponding amount issued by Party B is received.

Article 3 Delivery and Acceptance

3.1 Party B shall complete the supply, system integration and commissioning of all equipment by September 30, 2026, and meet the final inspection conditions. The details of equipment supply shall be subject to Annex II's Equipment Procurement List.

3.2 Party A shall organize the acceptance within 15 working days after receiving the acceptance application from Party B. For the acceptance standards, please refer to Annex I "Project Acceptance Specification".

3.3 Party B should provide complete technical documents, operation manuals and training services.

Article 4 Rights and Obligations of Party A

4.1 Party A shall provide the necessary on-site conditions, network environment and information support for Party B to carry out the integration work.

4.2 Party A has the right to supervise and inspect Party B's project progress, equipment quality and integration results.

4.3 Party A shall pay the contract price in a timely manner as agreed in this Contract.

Article 5 After-Sales Service

5.1 The warranty period of the system and equipment under this Contract is 24 months after the final inspection.

5.2 During the warranty period, Party B shall respond within 4 hours after receiving the repair notice from Party A, and arrive at the fault site for maintenance within 8 hours. It takes no more than 24 hours from the technician's fault handling to all troubleshooting and the whole system's full recovery to the normal state before the failure.

5.3 Party B should have a perfect after-sales service system, technical operation and maintenance team and spare parts library.

5.4 If Party B's maintenance service does not meet the requirements of Party A, Party A has the right to require Party B to pay a fine in accordance with the After-sales Maintenance Quality Assessment Measures.

Article 6 Confidentiality

6.1 Party B shall maintain strict confidentiality over Party A's trade secrets, network data and business information, and over the contents of this Contract, that Party B learns in connection with entering into or performing this Contract. Party B shall not disclose any such information to a third party without Party A's written consent. Party B shall pay Party A liquidated damages of RMB 300,000.00 (in words: Three Hundred Thousand Renminbi Only) for each breach of this clause. If the liquidated damages are insufficient to cover Party A's losses, Party B shall compensate the shortfall. Party A may also terminate this Contract.

6.2 The confidentiality obligations stipulated in this article shall not be limited by the term of this Contract and shall remain valid for five years after the termination of this Contract.

Article 7 Liability for Breach

7.1 Party B has one of the following breaches of contract and fails to correct it on the day of receiving the "Notice of Party B's Breach of Contract" issued by Party A or within the time extended by Party A's written approval, Party A has the right to unilaterally terminate part or all of the contract. In this case, it shall not affect Party A's claim against Party B.

7.2 If Party B delivers goods or provides services late, Party B shall pay Party A a late charge of 0.1% of the total Contract price per day of delay, capped at 10% of that price. Party A may terminate this Contract once the cumulative late charges reach 10% of the total Contract price.

7.3 If the results delivered by Party B do not meet the agreed standards and still do not meet the standards after two rectifications, Party A has the right to terminate this Contract. Party B shall refund all the money collected and pay Party A liquidated damages of 20% of the total contract price.

7.3 If Party A pays the contract price late, it shall pay liquidated damages to Party B at 0.03% of the outstanding amount for each day of delay.

Article 8 Force Majeure

If one party is unable to perform this Contract due to force majeure such as earthquakes, typhoons, floods, fires, wars, epidemics, government acts, etc., the encountering party shall notify the other party in writing in time and provide proof within a reasonable period of time. The two parties shall not be responsible for each other, but shall take necessary measures to prevent the expansion of losses.

Article 9 Anti-Bribery

In entering into and performing this Contract, both parties shall comply strictly with national laws and regulations against commercial bribery. Neither party may offer improper benefits in any form to, or accept them in any form from, the other party's personnel or any relevant third party. If either party breaches this clause, the non-breaching party may terminate this Contract and seek compensation for its losses.

Article 10 Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

10.1 The validity, interpretation, change, execution and dispute settlement of this Contract shall be governed by the laws of the People's Republic of China.

10.2 Any dispute arising out of or related to this Contract shall be resolved by amicable negotiations between the two parties; if the negotiation fails, either party shall have the right to file a lawsuit in the people's court with jurisdiction over the location of Party A.

Article 11 Effectiveness and Miscellaneous Provisions

11.1 This Contract takes effect on the date both parties affix their seals. It is executed in four counterparts of equal legal effect, two for each party.

11.2 For matters not covered by this Contract, the two parties may sign a written supplementary agreement separately, which shall have the same legal effect as this Contract.

11.3 Without the prior written consent of the other party, neither party shall assign the rights and obligations under this Contract to a third party.
