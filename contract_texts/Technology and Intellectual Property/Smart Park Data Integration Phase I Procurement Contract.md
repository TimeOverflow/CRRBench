# Zhongzhou Digital Technology Co., Ltd. Guangdong Branch

# 2026 Smart Park Data Integration Phase I Project

Procurement contract

Party A: Zhongzhou Digital Technology Co., Ltd. Guangdong Branch (hereinafter referred to as "purchaser")

Party B: Chengzhi Space Technology Services (Hangzhou) Co., Ltd. (Hereinafter referred to as "integrator")

In accordance with the Civil Code of the People's Republic of China and other relevant laws and regulations, Party A and Party B reached an agreement on the procurement of the first phase of the smart park data integration project after amicable consultation and signed this Contract.

Article 1 Project Scope

1. Party B provides Party A with equipment supply, system integration, software deployment and debugging services for the first phase of the smart park data integration project.

2. For specific equipment lists, software functions and technical parameters, please refer to Annex I "List of Equipment and Services".

3. Party B shall ensure that the equipment and software supplied meet the requirements of national standards, industry standards and Party A technical specifications.

Article 2 Contract Price and Payment

1. The total Contract price is RMB 2,860,000.00 (in words: Two Million Eight Hundred Sixty Thousand Renminbi Only), inclusive of tax and all costs, including equipment, software, integration, commissioning, training, and taxes and charges.

2. Payment progress: 30% after the contract is signed and effective, 40% after the equipment is accepted, 25% after the final inspection of the system, and 5% after the warranty period expires and there is no quality problem.

3. Party B shall issue a special VAT invoice of the corresponding amount to Party A before Party A pays each payment, and the applicable tax rate is 13%. Party B collection account information:

Article 3 Delivery and Acceptance

1. Party B shall complete the supply, system integration and commissioning of all equipment by October 31, 2026, and meet the final inspection conditions.

2. Party A shall organize the acceptance within 15 working days after receiving the acceptance application from Party B. The acceptance standards and procedures shall be implemented in accordance with Article 12, Paragraph 5 of this Contract.

3. Party B should provide complete technical documents, operation manuals and training services.

Article 4 Rights and Obligations of Party A

1. The owner shall provide the necessary on-site conditions, network environment and information support for Party B to carry out the integration work.

2. The purchaser has the right to supervise and inspect the project progress, equipment quality and integration results of Party B.

3. The purchaser shall pay the contract price in a timely manner as agreed in this Contract.

Article 5 Rights and Obligations of Party B

1. The integrator shall complete the equipment supply and system integration according to the agreed schedule and quality standards, and ensure the stability and safety of the results.

2. Party B shall be responsible for the management, safety and labor protection of its project personnel, and Party B shall be fully responsible for accidents caused by Party B personnel.

3. Party B shall properly keep the materials and commercial information provided by the purchaser and shall not be used for purposes other than this Contract without the written consent of the purchaser.

3. Party A has the right to transfer all or part of the rights and obligations under this Contract to its affiliates without the consent of Party B; without the prior written consent of Party A, Party B shall not transfer or subcontract all or part of the rights and obligations under this Contract to a third party, otherwise Party A has the right to terminate this Contract.

Article 6 After-Sales Service and Warranty

1. The warranty period of the system and equipment under this Contract is 24 months after final inspection.

2. During the warranty period, Party B shall respond within 4 hours after receiving the failure notice from Party A, troubleshoot within 24 hours, and restore normal operation of the system.

3. Party B should have a perfect after-sales service system, technical operation and maintenance team and spare parts warehouse.

4. Acceptance standards and processes: After the equipment arrives, unboxing inspection is carried out, and functional testing and performance testing are carried out after the system integration is completed. Only after all indicators meet the standards agreed in Annex I can the final inspection be passed.

Article 7 Liability for Breach

1. If Party B delivers goods or provides services late, Party B shall pay Party A liquidated damages equal to 0.1% of the total Contract price for each day of delay. If the delay exceeds thirty days, Party A may terminate this Contract, and Party B shall refund all amounts received and compensate Party A for its losses.

2. If the results delivered by Party B do not meet the agreed standards and still do not meet the standard after two rectifications, Party A has the right to terminate this Contract, and Party B shall pay Party A liquidated damages of 20% of the total price of this Contract.

3. If the after-sales service of Party B does not meet the requirements of Party A, Party A has the right to require Party B to pay a fine according to the assessment method.

4. If Party A pays the contract price late, it shall pay Party B liquidated damages of 0.03% of the outstanding amount for each day of delay.

Article 8 Confidentiality

Both parties have the obligation to keep confidential the trade secrets, technical information and undisclosed information of the other party learned from the signing and performance of this Contract, and shall not disclose them to third parties without the written consent of the other party, except as otherwise provided by laws and regulations or required by the regulatory authorities. The obligation of this article shall not be invalidated by the cancellation or termination of this Contract.

Article 9 Force Majeure

If one party is unable to perform this Contract due to force majeure such as earthquakes, typhoons, floods, fires, wars, epidemics, government acts, etc., the affected party shall notify the other party in writing in a timely manner and provide proof within a reasonable period of time, and the two parties shall not be responsible for each other.

Article 10 Anti-Bribery

In entering into and performing this Contract, both parties shall comply strictly with national laws and regulations against commercial bribery. Neither party may offer improper benefits in any form to, or accept them in any form from, the other party's personnel or any relevant third party. If either party breaches this clause, the non-breaching party may terminate this Contract and seek compensation for its losses.

Article 11 Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

1. The validity, interpretation, change, execution and dispute settlement of this Contract shall be governed by the laws of the People's Republic of China.

2. Any dispute arising out of or related to this Contract shall be resolved by amicable negotiations between the two parties; if the negotiation fails, either party shall have the right to file a lawsuit in the people's court with jurisdiction over the location of Party A.

Article 12 Effectiveness and Miscellaneous Provisions

1. This Contract takes effect on the date both parties affix their seals. It is executed in four counterparts of equal legal effect, two for each party.

2. For matters not covered by this Contract, the two parties may sign a written supplementary agreement separately, which shall have the same legal effect as this Contract.
