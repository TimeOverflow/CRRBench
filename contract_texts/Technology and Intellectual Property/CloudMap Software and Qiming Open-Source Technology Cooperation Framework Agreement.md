In view of the fact that Party A has technical and product advantages in the field of map and location services, and Party B has research and development capabilities in the field of open source information technology, the two sides have reached the following framework agreement on technical cooperation in accordance with the principles of equality, mutual benefit, honesty and credit:

I. The purpose and scope of cooperation

1. The two sides have established a long-term technical cooperation relationship and carried out cooperation in map data processing, open source component research and development, system integration and other fields.

2. This Agreement is a framework agreement, and the specific cooperation project, content, cost and delivery method shall be signed separately by the two parties.

3. The cooperation between the two parties shall abide by relevant laws and regulations, and shall not harm the legitimate rights and interests of any third party.

II. Mode of cooperation

1. Party A may entrust Party B to research and develop or provide technical services for specific technical modules, and Party B shall deliver the results as agreed in the specific project contract.

2. Party B can provide open source technical support, technical consultation and personnel training services to Party A.

3. Both parties can carry out joint research and development on technical topics of common interest, and the attribution of research and development results and revenue distribution shall be agreed upon by the specific project contract.

III. Intellectual Property

1. The intellectual property rights owned by all parties before the cooperation shall be owned by each other. Without the written consent of the other party, neither party shall use the other party's trademarks, patents, copyrights and other intellectual property rights.

2. The new intellectual property rights generated in the process of cooperation shall be agreed upon by the specific project contract; if they are not agreed, they shall be determined by negotiation between the two parties.

3. Party B guarantees that the technical services and results it provides do not infringe on the intellectual property rights of any third party. If a dispute is caused by this, Party B shall be responsible for resolving and bearing the corresponding responsibility.

IV. Fees and Settlement

1. The cost, settlement method and payment time of the specific project under this Agreement shall be stipulated in the specific project contract.

2. All expenses are calculated and settled in RMB. Party B shall issue a special VAT invoice to Party A as agreed, and the applicable tax rate shall be subject to the agreement of the specific project contract.

V. Confidentiality

1. Both parties have the obligation to keep confidential the trade secrets, technical information and undisclosed information of the other party learned through the signing and performance of this Agreement, and shall not disclose them to any third party without the written consent of the other party, except as otherwise provided by laws and regulations or required by the regulatory authorities.

2. If any party violates the confidentiality obligation and causes losses to the other party, it shall bear the corresponding liability for compensation in accordance with the law.

3. The confidentiality obligations stipulated in this article are not limited by the term of this Agreement and remain valid for three years after the termination of this Agreement.

VI. Liability for Breach

1. If any party violates this Agreement and causes losses to the other party, it shall bear the corresponding liability for compensation in accordance with the law.

2. If one party delays the performance of its main obligations and fails to perform within 30 days after the written reminder of the other party, the other party has the right to terminate this Agreement and claim compensation for losses.

VII. Force Majeure

If one party is unable to perform this Agreement due to force majeure such as earthquakes, typhoons, floods, fires, wars, epidemics, government acts, etc., the party shall notify the other party in writing in a timely manner and provide proof within a reasonable period of time, and the two parties shall not be responsible for each other.

VIII. Anti-Bribery

In entering into and performing this Agreement, both parties shall comply strictly with national laws and regulations against commercial bribery. Neither party may offer improper benefits in any form to, or accept them in any form from, the other party's personnel or any relevant third party. If either party breaches this clause, the non-breaching party may terminate this Agreement and seek compensation for its losses.

IX. Term and Termination

1. This Agreement shall be valid for three years from the date of signing by both parties. Thirty days before the expiration of the term, the two parties can negotiate for renewal.

2. This Agreement may be terminated by mutual agreement between the two parties. If one party breaches the contract and has not been corrected within 30 days after a written reminder, the non-breaching party shall have the right to terminate this Agreement.

3. The cancellation or termination of this Agreement shall not affect the rights and obligations incurred by both parties under this Agreement and the specific project contract before the termination date.

X. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

1. The validity, interpretation, change, execution and dispute settlement of this Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the People's Republic of China.

2. Any dispute arising out of or related to this Agreement shall be resolved by amicable negotiations between the two parties; if the negotiation fails, either party has the right to file a lawsuit in the people's court with jurisdiction over the location of Party A.

XI. Miscellaneous

1. For matters not covered by this Agreement, the two parties may sign a written supplementary agreement separately, and the supplementary agreement shall have the same legal effect as this Agreement.

2. Without the prior written consent of the other party, neither party shall transfer the rights and obligations under this Agreement to a third party.

3. This Agreement shall be made in duplicate, one copy for each of Party A and Party B, which shall have the same legal effect and shall take effect from the date of the seal of both parties.
